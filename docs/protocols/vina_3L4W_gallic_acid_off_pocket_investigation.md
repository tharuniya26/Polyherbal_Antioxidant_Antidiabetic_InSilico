# Step 64 — Read-only investigation of gallic-acid off-pocket ranking in 3L4W

**Decision B: retain the Step 63 result as INCONCLUSIVE and move on.** In the frozen dry/no-NAG models, all 20 rank-1 gallic-acid poses occupy the same off-MIG-pocket surface location at the enlarged box's Z− face. Every run also sampled a lower-ranked MIG-pocket pose. The original 3L4W coordinates place six crystallographic water oxygens within 1.8 Å of every off-pocket rank-1 ligand, an important omission from the dry model. These observations make the rank-1 site and its small apparent scoring advantage unreliable as a physical binding-site assignment. A smaller MIG-localized search would exclude the competing site by construction; it would not resolve the missing-water chemistry or establish why Vina prefers it. No further docking is recommended from this investigation.

## Rank-1 site and first MIG-pocket alternative

The deposited MIG heavy-atom centroid and box center are `(45.38664286, 92.35157143, 34.82200000)` Å. The enlarged 30 × 24 × 24 Å search box has Z− face at **22.822 Å**. All 20 rank-1 poses are off pocket: their frozen ten-heavy-atom core centroids lie **15.185–15.282 Å** from the MIG centroid, with **zero** core atoms within the frozen 4 Å MIG-neighborhood test. Their ligand-heavy centroids cluster around `(56.9, 89.2, 25.8)` Å. In every rank-1 pose, source-SDF atom **8, a phenolic oxygen**, approaches the Z− face to **0.008–0.067 Å**. Thus the search boundary constrains these saved poses, although the data alone cannot show where the ligand would go in a larger box. The Step 63 severe-clash test against the **dry receptor** found no severe protein or internal clash.

The off-pocket placement lies against a recurring group of protein surface residues, including **His183, Trp552, Glu182, Leu540, Gly556 and Pro206**. His183 is approximately **2.70–2.74 Å** and Trp552 approximately **2.71–2.84 Å** from a ligand heavy atom in representative saved poses. Across runs, 9–11 protein residues have a heavy atom within 4 Å of rank 1. This is a geometrically coherent surface contact region; calling it a bound groove would overstate the evidence because it sits on the search limit and was scored after removal of nearby crystal waters. The nearest deposited MIG atom is over 10 Å away in a representative rank-1 pose, while deposited linked NAG A2005 is about 9.5 Å away. These are geometry observations, not proof that the glycan is irrelevant in solution.

The first saved pose meeting the **unchanged** MIG-pocket and dry-receptor clash rules occurs at rank **3 in 18 runs** and rank **2 in two runs** (carboxylate/Asp542H seeds 1101 and 2202). These alternatives have a core centroid **0.709–0.802 Å** from the MIG centroid and **100%** core-atom overlap under the frozen 4 Å MIG test. Their nearest box-face clearances are **9.094–9.492 Å**, with no face warning. They approach the deposited MIG site near Asp327, Asp542, His600, Arg526, Trp406 and Asp443; their minimum protein-heavy distances are about 2.6 Å in representative poses. This is catalytic-pocket localization, not evidence of correct hydrogen orientations or enzyme inhibition.

## Original deposited waters and structural limits

Read-only comparison with the **unchanged original** 3L4W PDB finds that every rank-1 pose passes within **0.545–0.640 Å** of its nearest crystallographic water oxygen and within **1.8 Å of the same six water sites**: HOH A4031, A4097, A4107, A4173, A4281 and A4320. In a representative neutral/Asp542− seed-1101 rank-1 pose, HOH A4097 is 0.550 Å away and HOH A4320 is 0.607 Å away. These deposited waters each have occupancy **1.00** in the original PDB. The first MIG-pocket alternatives have **no deposited water oxygen within 1.8 Å**; their nearest water is HOH A4004 at **2.684–3.035 Å** across all 20 runs. The original water positions are not part of either dry docking receptor, so these contacts were not penalized in Step 63.

This is direct coordinate evidence that the *saved off-pocket placements conflict with the deposited hydration pattern*. It does **not** establish that all six waters are retained, oriented the same way, or cannot be displaced by a real ligand. Conversely, deleting them without modelling displacement makes this surface region artificially available in the tested receptor. Static water orientations and Vina's standard scoring would also require separate chemical justification before a water-retaining model could be trusted. The dry/no-NAG model, provisional Asp542 and His600 protonation, unknown gallic-acid bound microstate, search-edge placement and Vina scoring all remain limitations. Native-MIG recovery in the 30 × 24 × 24 Å box was 10/10 rank-1 poses at ≤2 Å, but that is a **MIG pose-recovery control**, not validation of gallic-acid site choice or score ordering.

## Decision and next research task

**B — retain the inconclusive gallic-acid result and move on.** The existing experiment already shows that the MIG pocket is sampled in every seed and that rank 1 instead favors a box-edge location occupied by deposited water sites in the original crystal. A MIG-restricted box would mechanically suppress that competing location; a water-retaining Vina model currently lacks a validated treatment of these specific waters and their hydrogen orientations. Neither would presently resolve physical site preference. No new gallic-acid docking search is justified from these data, and the Step 63 outcome must remain intact.

For one future candidate from another plant, the **next executable task is an input-qualification and evidence review, with no docking**. For *Boerhavia diffusa* caffeic acid (BD01/LIG03), verify a parent-compound analytical assignment in the correct species and **isolated water-extracted leaves** or explicitly retain the [mixed-aerial-part boiled-water evidence](https://doi.org/10.3390/antiox10122003) as conditional; keep the [methanolic-leaf tri-TMS derivative](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jtcme.2019.02.002) separate from underivatized caffeic acid; verify whether the source establishes the **E/trans versus Z** double bond; and check CID 689043, the stereodefined reference graph, SDF/PDBQT hashes, pH-6.5 carboxylate hypothesis and atom mapping. For *Vitex negundo* agnuside (VN03/LIG09), inspect the [full primary water-only leaf chromatogram and analytical method](https://doi.org/10.1007/s42250-022-00383-8) rather than relying on an abstract or a [methanol-first aqueous partition](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpha.2013.09.008); verify the CID 442416 reference stereocentres against the source assignment, glycoside graph, SDF/PDBQT hashes, pH-6.5 microstates and flexibility. **Neither compound was analytically confirmed in this project's original aqueous extracts.** Select at most one for a separately frozen pose-only setup only after these chemical and botanical gates are documented; do not expand a five-plant docking panel on the basis of Step 63 scores.
