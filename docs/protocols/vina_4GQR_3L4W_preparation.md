# Preliminary Vina preparation of 4GQR/MYC and 3L4W/MIG

**Status, 12 October 2026:** official crystallographic sources and native reference ligands verified; two **local-pocket sensitivity receptor PDBQTs** generated. **Neither complex is cleared for controlled diagnostic redocking or scoring interpretation.** No docking, score-only run, RMSD calculation, protein minimization, ADMET analysis, or production phytochemical docking was performed. The machine-readable [preparation results](../../data/metadata/vina_alternative_receptor_preparation_results.csv) and [reproducibility script](../../scripts/prepare_vina_alternative_validation.py) record paths, checksums and decisions.

## Official sources and immutable reference structures

The [4GQR RCSB entry](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/4GQR) is human pancreatic α-amylase with noncovalent active-site myricetin (**MYC**), determined at **1.20 Å**. The [primary structural paper](https://doi.org/10.1021/jm301273u) explicitly describes myricetin's catalytic-site binding. The [3L4W RCSB entry](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/3L4W) is the human intestinal maltase-glucoamylase **N-terminal catalytic domain** with miglitol (**MIG**) at **2.00 Å**, from the [primary ntMGAM structural study](https://doi.org/10.1021/bi9016457). These complexes validate different receptors; one cannot substitute for the other's native-pose test.

## Native inhibitor recovery and PDBQT checks

MYC A504 and MIG A1001 were selected by exact deposited residue identity. Every heavy atom name, element and coordinate in the PDB matched the corresponding official mmCIF record; no selected atom had zero occupancy. Chemical bonds came from the official CCD definitions, and RDKit-reconstructed formula, connectivity and stereochemistry matched the CCD stereochemical reference. The [4GQR validation report](https://files.rcsb.org/validation/view/4gqr_full_validation.pdf) confirms all **23/23 MYC heavy atoms** but notes ligand geometry outliers worth inspecting. The [3L4W validation report](https://files.rcsb.org/validation/view/3l4w_full_validation.pdf) confirms **14/14 MIG heavy atoms** and no chirality outlier.

RDKit added hydrogens while preserving all crystallographic **heavy** coordinates. Meeko used `MoleculePreparation(add_index_map=True, charge_model='gasteiger')` and `PDBQTWriterLegacy`; its output was read back with `PDBQTMolecule`/`RDKitMolCreate`. The PDBQT torsion counts exceed the earlier RDKit rotatable-bond descriptor estimates (MYC 1, MIG 3) because the programs count flexibility differently, including some X–H rotations. These files are **neutral CCD-reference microstates** and preserve the crystal poses for later heavy-atom RMSD. MYC phenol ionization/tautomerism and especially MIG amine protonation at pH 6.5 remain unassessed. The reference PDBQTs must **not** be used as the sole starting coordinates for a claimed redocking recovery test.

## Receptor chemistry and derived local models

The unchanged ligand-free working copies retain deposited non-native material: [4GQR working PDB](../../structures/proteins/prepared/4GQR_vina_validation_working_no_native.pdb) and [3L4W working PDB](../../structures/proteins/prepared/3L4W_vina_validation_working_no_native.pdb). Neither full native receptor was converted to PDBQT, because linked glycan preparation remains unsupported by the validated local Meeko template workflow. The two **new, explicitly glycan-excluded local-pocket models** are [4GQR protonated PDB](../../structures/proteins/prepared/4GQR_validation_local_noNAG_pH7p5.pdb) / [PDBQT](../../structures/proteins/vina_prepared/4GQR_validation_local_noNAG_Meeko0.8.0.pdbqt) and [3L4W protonated PDB](../../structures/proteins/prepared/3L4W_validation_local_noNAG_pH6p5.pdb) / [PDBQT](../../structures/proteins/vina_prepared/3L4W_validation_local_noNAG_Meeko0.8.0.pdbqt). They are sensitivity approximations, **not full native receptor preparations**.

Hydrogen coordinates and all protonation assignments above are **computational**, not established by X-ray diffraction. PROPKA/PDB2PQR are absent from this project environment, so no site-specific pKa calculations were run. The generated water O–H distances are **1.176–1.190 Å** (4GQR) and **1.174–1.200 Å** (3L4W); water hydrogen geometry and the choice to fix these waters need review. The local model's omission of protein-linked NAG restores ordinary Asn amide hydrogens and must not be described as retaining native glycosylation.

The nearest deposited inhibitor–protein heavy-atom distances are **2.403 Å** (MYC O25–Asp197 OD2) and **2.538 Å** (MIG OAE–Asp327 OD2), with no pair below 2.0 Å. These are contact checks, not assigned hydrogen bonds. Retained 4GQR HOH1244 has a high deposited B factor (**72.39 Å²**), so its inclusion by the 4 Å rule needs an explicit water-sensitivity comparison.

## Vina compatibility and decision

The receptor PDBQTs were checked by Meeko readback for finite coordinates/charges and matched against all retained source heavy-atom coordinates. The native ligand PDBQTs passed independent graph/stereo and coordinate roundtrips. **No Vina score, search or redocking was run.** Actual Vina parsing of these *new* receptor–ligand pairs and box definitions remains a future input preflight, followed by multiple independent pose-recovery runs and correctly mapped RMSD only if chemistry is accepted.

- **4GQR:** **blocked for physically interpretable standard-Vina validation**. The PDBQT contains Ca/Cl with the intended charge fields, but [standard Vina ignores input partial charges](https://autodock-vina.readthedocs.io/en/stable/faq.html) and lacks a validated treatment of their coordination/electrostatics. PCA conversion and remote-glycan omission solve file-preparation obstacles, not this scoring limitation. Catalytic/MYC microstates and fixed waters also remain provisional. A smaller native ligand makes an eventual *diagnostic pose-recovery test* attractive, but not validated yet.
- **3L4W:** **not yet ready for controlled diagnostic redocking**. It avoids the Ca/Cl issue and its MIG pose is complete, but the current receptor is a **NAG-free local sensitivity model** with provisional catalytic states, a doubly protonated His600 hypothesis, the unresolved Gln837 gap/remote terminus, and fixed-water choices. MIG's neutral CCD amine may be inappropriate at the crystal pH. Determine and label receptor and MIG microstates, assess the linked glycans' effect or validate a chemically supported glycan-retaining alternative, and run a Vina input preflight before any controlled search.

Preserve the full original and working models. For either target, a successful file conversion or future low heavy-atom RMSD would establish at most pose recovery under a specified model. It would not validate enzyme inhibition, binding affinity, any project extract, or cross-receptor transferability.

## Reproduction

Run from the repository root with the existing project environment:

```powershell
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe scripts\prepare_vina_alternative_validation.py
```

The script pins the six official source hashes, preserves existing outputs, reconstructs MYC/MIG from CCD atom names and bonds, compares PDB/mmCIF heavy coordinates, adds only labelled provisional hydrogens, selects local waters by a fixed rule, invokes Meeko, and checks PDBQT readback. The [CSV](../../data/metadata/vina_alternative_receptor_preparation_results.csv) contains hashes of all generated SDF/PDB/PDBQT files and atom maps, software versions, water IDs and the remaining blockers. A changed official deposition or changed local file causes a fail-closed error for that complex and requires a new scientific audit, not silent replacement.
