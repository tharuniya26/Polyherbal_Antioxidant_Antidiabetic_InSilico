# Initial structural assessment of 2QMJ and 1XD0

**Assessment date:** 11 October 2026  
**Status:** Original RCSB PDB files retrieved and inspected. No receptor has been prepared, minimized, redocked, or validated for GOLD.

## Scope and provenance

These two human digestive-enzyme structures were selected in [molecular_target_selection.md](molecular_target_selection.md) and [target_candidates.csv](../../data/metadata/target_candidates.csv). They represent mechanisms of dietary carbohydrate digestion relevant to future antidiabetic-related docking. The project's original glucose adsorption and yeast uptake assays **did not test inhibition of either enzyme**. The [ligand preparation results](../../data/metadata/ligand_preparation_results.csv) describe ten neutral phytochemical **reference structures**; their preparation does not show that the compounds were present in the original aqueous extracts or will bind either receptor.

Both files were downloaded without alteration from the official RCSB coordinate service on 11 October 2026. The [machine-readable audit](../../data/metadata/receptor_structure_audit.csv) records the checksums and main findings.

## 2QMJ: human N-terminal maltase-glucoamylase

The [RCSB entry](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/2QMJ) identifies **human intestinal MGAM**, expressed in *Drosophila melanogaster*, with **X-ray diffraction at 1.90 Å** and no protein mutation. Its biological assembly is a **monomer**; the deposited construct covers the N-terminal catalytic subunit, not full-length membrane-bound MGAM. The entry has **870 deposited construct residues and 863 modeled residues**. Author protein chain **A** is the only protein chain. `REMARK 465` lists chain-A positions **1–6 and 837** as unobserved; these are construct numbers, not UniProt full-length positions. The candidate catalytic residues are present in the coordinate file. The structure's reported R-free is **0.215**. [RCSB 2QMJ](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/2QMJ); [Sim et al. (2008)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jmb.2007.10.069).

### Inhibitor and other nonprotein records

The bound inhibitor is the RCSB biologically interesting molecule **alpha-acarbose, BIRD `PRD_900007`**, represented in the author PDB as a **linked oligosaccharide on chain B**, not as one ordinary `HETATM` residue. It comprises **GLC B 1, GLC B 2, and AC1 B 3** (44 deposited heavy atoms). `LINK` records join GLC B1 O4 to GLC B2 C1, then GLC B2 O4 to AC1 B3 C1. AC1 is the acarviosine-containing component; [RCSB's 2QMJ entry](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/2QMJ) identifies the whole branched entity as alpha-acarbose. Splitting AC1, GLC, and GLC into three independent GOLD ligands would invalidate a redocking reference. Their atom occupancy fields are 1.00. Median deposited B factors differ across the inhibitor: **AC1 27.2 Å², GLC B2 46.2 Å², and GLC B1 56.6 Å²**. This gradient calls for density/pose-quality review, particularly at the distal sugar end; occupancy and B factors alone do not establish local map quality.

Other hetero records are **four NAG units** (N-linked glycosylation, including a two-unit glycan on author chain X), **nine glycerols (GOL)**, **one sulfate (SO4 A4001)**, and **626 waters (HOH)**. NAG A2005 is linked to Asn209 and lies **3.9 Å** from the inhibitor by minimum heavy-atom distance. Ten water oxygens lie within **4 Å** of the inhibitor and 17 within 6 Å. The sulfate is about **49 Å** from the inhibitor, so it is not a deposited catalytic metal or a reason to remove site waters. The file contains **A/B alternate conformations** for Arg68, Cys479, Glu661, Asn741, Lys762, and NAG A2001; Cys479 is the closest of these to acarbose at about **10.2 Å**. No alternative protein conformation was detected within the 4 Å ligand-contact set. A later prepared copy must make any altloc choice explicit.

### Catalytic pocket and chain choice

The catalytic nucleophile is **Asp443** and the acid/base residue is **Asp542**, using 2QMJ construct numbering. RCSB's [MGAM–acarbose structure explanation](https://pdb101.rcsb.org/global-health/diabetes-mellitus/drugs/alpha-glucosidase-inhibitors/drug/acarbose/acarbose) places **Asp203, Thr205, Asp327, Arg526, Asp542, and His600** in the inhibitor-binding network; it also distinguishes the catalytic role of Asp443 from a claimed direct inhibitor hydrogen bond. The deposited coordinates place all these residues within roughly **2.5–3.4 Å minimum heavy-atom distance** of some inhibitor atom. This geometric check locates the site; it does not assign bond energetics or protonation. The [primary structure paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jmb.2007.10.069) describes primary interactions around the acarvosine unit and fewer protein contacts to distal glycone rings.

Use **author protein chain A** for a later GOLD receptor copy. Retain an unchanged copy of the **entire linked chain-B acarbose entity** as the experimental reference before any removal. Define the catalytic cleft from the intact inhibitor coordinates and catalytic residues, then check whether a site sphere centered on the full oligosaccharide is unnecessarily expanded by distal sugars. Review NAG A2005 and each local water before deciding whether to retain them. There is one protein chain in the asymmetric unit and biological assembly. A Gemmi symmetry search found **no inhibitor–protein crystal-mate contact within 4 Å**; crystal packing elsewhere is present and does not establish an additional biological protein chain.

## 1XD0: human pancreatic alpha-amylase

The [RCSB entry](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/1XD0) identifies **human pancreatic alpha-amylase (AMY2A)**, expressed in *Pichia/Komagataella pastoris*, with **X-ray diffraction at 2.00 Å** and no protein mutation. Its author chain **A** is the sole protein chain and the assigned biological assembly is a **monomer**. RCSB reports **496 deposited and 496 modeled mature-protein residues**. The N-terminal residue **PCA A1** is a pyroglutamate linked by a peptide bond to Tyr A2; it appears as a `HETATM`/modified residue and must not be discarded as a free ligand. There is no `REMARK 465` missing-residue list. This does not prove every side-chain atom or hydrogen is complete. Reported R-free is **0.199**. [RCSB 1XD0](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/1XD0); [Li et al. (2005)](https://doi.org/10.1021/bi048334e).

### Bound product, ions, and waters

The deposited reference is **ARE A501**, the RCSB [chemical component ARE](https://www.rcsb.org/ligand/ARE), named **acarbose-derived pentasaccharide**. It is a **rearrangement product**, not intact acarbose. The PDB has 55 ARE heavy atoms, all with occupancy 1.00; their median B factor is **16.9 Å²**. The [primary structure paper](https://doi.org/10.1021/bi048334e) examines this acarbose rearrangement mechanism. Redocking ARE can test recovery of **ARE's deposited product pose**. It cannot by itself validate docking of intact acarbose or the phytochemicals.

The file also contains **NAG A499** linked to Asn461, **one Ca²⁺ (CA A498)**, **one Cl⁻ (CL A500)**, and **142 HOH waters**. `LINK` and `REMARK 620` records show calcium coordination by Asn100, Arg158 backbone O, Asp167, His201 backbone O, and **waters HOH A536 and A546**. Calcium is about **10.1 Å** from ARE and chloride about **5.5 Å** by minimum heavy-atom distance. Four water oxygens are within 4 Å of ARE. Calcium and its coordinating waters require a joint decision; chloride is a functional amylase ion and must not be removed simply because it is a `HETATM`. No alternate atom locations were detected in the deposited 1XD0 coordinate file. [RCSB 1XD0](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/1XD0); [chloride activation study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2279296/).

### Catalytic pocket and crystal-mate contact

**Asp197** is the catalytic nucleophile; **Glu233** acts as general acid/base; **Asp300** contributes to the catalytic environment and water/substrate interactions. These roles are supported by [mutagenesis and structural work](https://doi.org/10.1021/bi011821z) and the [1XD0 structure paper](https://doi.org/10.1021/bi048334e). Their minimum heavy-atom distances to ARE in the deposited model are **2.92, 2.59, and 2.59 Å**, respectively. Nearby cleft residues include His201, Lys200, and Glu240. Define a future GOLD site using ARE's observed coordinates **and** this catalytic pocket rather than a residue-name list alone.

Although only one protein chain is deposited and the biological assembly is monomeric, a **Gemmi 0.7.3 symmetry-mate search** found five atom-pair contacts under 4 Å between ARE atoms **O3A/C3A** and a crystallographic copy of **Asp433/Trp434** (nearest contact **2.72 Å**). The symmetry mate is a **crystal packing neighbor**, not an additional biological subunit to add automatically to GOLD. It may influence that part of the ARE pose; full-ligand pose recovery in an isolated-chain receptor therefore needs cautious interpretation. The catalytic core and crystal-contacted region should both be inspected during later validation. The original coordinates remain unchanged.
