# 1XD0 AutoDock Vina input preflight

**Assessment date:** 12 October 2026. **Decision:** both receptor PDBQTs pass a non-production Vina 1.2.7 parsing and `--score_only` test, but **both are blocked for scientifically interpretable native-ligand redocking with the standard Vina scoring function**. No pose search, minimization, redocking, production docking, ADMET, or activity prediction was performed. The [results CSV](../../data/metadata/vina_1xd0_input_preflight_results.csv) and [preflight script](../../scripts/preflight_1xd0_vina_inputs.py) make the checks reproducible. The numeric score-only outputs are diagnostic evidence that inputs were accepted; they are **not binding affinities or results for the project phytochemicals**.

## ARE identity and search box

ARE is the **deposited acarbose-derived pentasaccharide** in 1XD0, not intact acarbose. Its original SDF and Meeko PDBQT reconstruct the same isomeric RDKit molecular graph; the reference has **55 heavy atoms, 28 PDBQT torsions and formal charge zero**. Its nitrogen is represented as a **neutral secondary amine with one bonded hydrogen**. Whether that microstate is appropriate at the intended assay/docking pH remains unresolved; changing it would require an explicit alternative ligand model, never a silent replacement of the crystal reference. The PDBQT stereochemistry and heavy-coordinate mapping passed the earlier [native reference validation](vina_native_redocking_plan.md).

The reference heavy-atom centroid is **(10.441, 15.880, 40.721) Å**. A reproducible box of **32 × 23 × 26 Å** has at least approximately 6 Å clearance around the full deposited heavy-atom extent. The x dimension is large because ARE is extended; the [Vina FAQ](https://autodock-vina.readthedocs.io/en/stable/faq.html) advises more search effort for large boxes. This box is a geometric preflight setting, not a validated docking search protocol. No non-native ligand starting pose was made.

## PCA, ions and catalytic residues

PCA A1 retains its eight heavy atoms and the deposited **PCA C–Tyr2 N distance of 1.357 Å**. The linked S-stereochemistry [custom template](../../docking/configs/vina_templates/PCA_C_terminal_Meeko_0.8.0.json) was validated against the official [RCSB PCA definition](https://files.rcsb.org/ligands/download/PCA.cif). PDBQT is a rigid receptor atom format and does not itself serialize a peptide bond; the template, retained atom identities, charge context and C–N geometry support the linkage interpretation. PCA was not detached, deleted, or substituted.

Both PDBQTs contain **Ca A498** as `Ca`, charge field **+2.000**, and **Cl A500** as `Cl`, charge field **−1.000**. Ca-coordinating HOH A536/A546 are retained. Calcium is **10.077 Å** from the nearest ARE heavy atom and chloride **5.541 Å** away, so chloride is close enough to warrant scoring scrutiny. The original [structural audit](../../data/metadata/receptor_structure_audit.csv) records the rest of the calcium coordination shell. No ion was deleted to make the diagnostic run succeed.

The receptor PDBQTs give **Asp197 −1, Glu233 −1, Asp300 −1 and neutral His201 with HD1** (rounded residue partial-charge sums). These are the earlier ligand-free OpenMM pH 7 assignments, not experimentally established catalytic states. Their hydrogen-bond donor/acceptor typing may change under alternative microstates. The score-only check cannot select the correct catalytic protonation.

## Why Vina acceptance does not clear scoring

The [Vina FAQ](https://autodock-vina.readthedocs.io/en/stable/faq.html) states that its standard scoring function ignores user-supplied partial charges and uses hydrogens primarily to determine donor/acceptor classification. In the [Vina 1.2.7 atom-typing source](https://github.com/ccsb-scripps/AutoDock-Vina/blob/v1.2.7/src/lib/model.cpp), **all chlorine atoms** map to the hydrophobic `XS_TYPE_Cl_H` class and **metals** to a generic `XS_TYPE_Met_D` class. Therefore the input's **Cl−** field is parsed yet scored as hydrophobic chlorine, and the **Ca²⁺** field does not yield an explicit calcium electrostatic/coordination model. This source-based interpretation is supported by the successful local parse; it is not proof that those interactions are physically valid. The official [atom-type definitions](https://github.com/ccsb-scripps/AutoDock-Vina/blob/v1.2.7/src/lib/atom_constants.h) include both `Ca` and `Cl` as syntactically accepted PDBQT types.

Every retained water is `OW:OA` plus two `HD` atoms, so Vina can classify its oxygen as both acceptor and donor. Its standard scoring is **united-atom and nondirectional** for the [hydrogen-bond term](https://github.com/ccsb-scripps/AutoDock-Vina/blob/v1.2.7/src/lib/potentials.h); these receptor waters remain fixed, cannot be displaced by ARE, and have no water entropy or rearrangement penalty. The [official hydrated-docking protocol](https://autodock-vina.readthedocs.io/en/stable/docking_hydrated.html) is a different, AD4-calibrated method and explicitly discourages applying that protocol to Vina/Vinardo. The all-water model contains many remote waters; the site-water model includes four waters within 4 Å of ARE plus HOH536/546, but omits other waters around 4.7–5.1 Å that could still affect a scoring grid. Neither selection has been validated as the correct fixed-water network.

An additional geometry check found **water O–H lengths of 1.167–1.194 Å** (all-water) and **1.177–1.196 Å** (site-water), with H–O–H angles around 103–107°. These bond lengths came from the earlier protonated PDB inputs, not from PDBQT conversion, and are substantially longer than the **0.9572 Å** reference bond in OpenMM's [TIP3P definition](https://github.com/openmm/openmm/blob/master/wrappers/python/openmm/app/data/tip3p.xml). Vina does not use the H coordinates as directional interaction energies, but this discrepancy means their preparation geometry needs correction and review before treating fixed waters as chemically sound.

## Steric and crystallographic checks

For both variants the nearest **ARE–protein heavy-atom** distance is **2.551 Å**, the nearest **ARE–water O** distance **2.859 Å**, and there are **zero ARE–protein heavy-atom pairs below 2.0 Å**. These checks find no gross overlap in the deposited pose; they do not validate all local contacts or the chosen protonation. The earlier [1XD0 structural audit](../../data/metadata/receptor_structure_audit.csv) found contacts between ARE O3A/C3A and crystal-mate Asp433/Trp434 within 4 Å. Those lattice contacts are absent from the isolated chain-A docking receptor, limiting interpretation of full-pentasaccharide pose recovery.

## Decision and next scientific step

**Both existing 1XD0 variants remain blocked for physically interpretable native-ligand redocking with `--scoring vina`.** Parsing and diagnostic scoring succeed, but the charge-insensitive treatment of retained chloride/calcium, the provisional catalytic and ARE microstates, and unvalidated fixed-water geometry/selection are material. Do not interpret the two diagnostic values as affinities or use them to rank receptors.

A defensible next model would retain PCA, Ca and Cl; correct and inspect water geometry; predefine a small set of water-network alternatives based on deposited contacts, occupancy and calcium coordination; and assess an electrostatics-aware scoring route such as **AutoDock4 maps** with validated ion parameters. That route itself needs a fresh preflight and native-pose validation. If a chloride-free sensitivity model is ever considered, its removal must have a structural/biological justification independent of software convenience, and the original ion-retaining receptor must remain available. No such alternative receptor was generated in this preflight.
