# 3L4W ntMGAM readiness for exploratory phytochemical docking

**Step 56 decision:** **Conditionally ready for a restricted pose-only pilot; not ready for production phytochemical scoring or potency ranking.** The exact [3L4W/MIG experimental complex](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/3L4W) supports target relevance. Five-seed diagnostic redocking recovered the deposited MIG heavy-atom pose at rank 1 within **2.0 Å** for neutral and one modeled amine-protonated MIG on the dry/no-NAG Asp542− receptor, and for both ligands on one dry/no-NAG Asp542H(OD2) sensitivity receptor. This is **pose recovery for four tested receptor–ligand combinations**, not validation of solution protonation, enzyme inhibition by phytochemicals, binding free energy, or the full glycosylated receptor.

## What has already passed

The [initial native-redocking report](vina_3L4W_redocking_report.md), [E64/ligand-sensitivity assessment](vina_3L4W_validation_assessment.md), [receptor-sensitivity report](vina_3L4W_receptor_sensitivity.md), and [run summary](../../data/metadata/vina_target_validation_summary.csv) reconcile to **25/25 completed 3L4W diagnostic runs**, **0 failures**, and **492 saved poses**. This total includes five E32 neutral runs and four five-seed E64 conditions. All 25 rank-1 native-MIG poses passed the prespecified ≤2 Å screen. The E64 neutral rank-1 mode was stable relative to E32, so no E128 repetition is needed. The deposited 14-heavy-atom MIG map and crystal-frame RMSD procedure were checked, as were modeled charged-N geometry and the Asp542H receptor's unchanged heavy atoms and Meeko atom types. These checks are specific to native MIG; they do not validate an arbitrary phytochemical structure or Vina score.

## Remaining receptor and scoring limits

| Issue | Direct observation and current boundary | Consequence for the next phase |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Linked glycans** | Six deposited NAG units are covalently linked; the nearest, A2005, is **16.409 Å from MIG** but **10.316 Å from Asp443/Asp542/His600**. The current dry receptors omit all six because a chemically complete Meeko polymer template was not validated. | The models preserve local deposited protein heavy coordinates, but glycan-dependent conformational/electrostatic effects remain unknown. A no-NAG **local-pocket** pilot can be labelled as such; claims about a fully native receptor, binding energetics or therapeutic activity fail this gate. A glycan-retaining model would require validated link chemistry, stereochemistry, charge typing and its own redocking. |
| **Active-site waters** | HOH A4002/A4004 contact the native site. The prepared two-water model has uncertain water-H directions and static occupancy. Ordinary rigid Vina does not sample exchange. | Keep the audited **dry** baseline for a restricted pilot and state the omission. If a candidate's pose interpretation depends on water bridges, hold that interpretation until a chemically justified and separately validated water method exists. Do not insert the earlier static-water PDBQT as if it settled solvent sensitivity. |
| **Catalytic protonation** | Asp443−/His600-HID were held fixed; Asp542− and one Asp542H(OD2) orientation were tested. Apo versus MIG-bound PROPKA predictions for Asp542 diverge. A ligand-facing OD1 acid orientation and His600-HIE were not converted to nonclashing models. | Treat both tested Asp542 states as hypotheses. A pilot must report whether a pose/contact pattern persists across them. Do not choose whichever state gives a favorable score. No further protonation testing is required merely to repeat the same pose screen; a new state requires specific chemical evidence and joint H-geometry QC. |
| **MIG protonation and pose ranking** | Neutral and one modeled amine-+1 MIG passed; the crystal does not assign the amine hydrogen/charge. In all charged E64 runs a more crystal-like ~0.61–0.62 Å pose ranked **second**. | Use native MIG only as a pose-placement positive control. A Vina rank-1 score is not an experimentally validated preference for a protonation state or the closest crystal pose. Do not convert phytochemical score differences into affinities or inhibition constants. |
| **Other structure limits** | Gln837 and other segments are unobserved; Gln837 flanks lie >52 Å from MIG. D772N is deposited. No invented coordinates were added. | Record the chain break/mutation and keep the same receptor heavy coordinates. If a proposed binding mode reaches an unmodeled region, it falls outside the qualified local site. |

The [chemistry review](vina_3L4W_chemistry_review.md) provides deposited distances, water IDs, missing-residue details and the preparation pH **6.5**. This is crystallization pH, not a measured bound-state pKa or a universal intestinal pH. Standard Vina's [official FAQ](https://autodock-vina.readthedocs.io/en/stable/faq.html) says supplied partial charges are ignored by its scoring function; apparent robustness across charged states does not establish correct proton-coupled energetics.

## Minimum gates for a defensible restricted pilot

These are **prospective pass/fail checks**, not requests to repeat completed native-MIG experiments.

| Gate | Pass criterion | Fail/hold condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Freeze receptor scope** | Verify SHA256, atom counts, coordinates, dry/no-NAG exclusions and pH-6.5 microstate labels for **both** tested Asp542− and Asp542H(OD2) PDBQTs; predeclare the MIG-derived pocket and a pose-only question. | Any input differs from its audit, an unrecorded deletion/protonation occurs, or results are described as the complete glycosylated enzyme. |
| **2. Qualify each ligand** | For each chosen structure, confirm the PubChem-backed graph and stereo against its [association record](../../data/compounds/final_preliminary_ligand_library.csv); assign and document pH-6.5 ionization/tautomer, conformer choice, PDBQT readback and hash. Keep literature evidence tier separate from docking readiness. | Unresolved exact identity/stereochemistry is silently selected, an acid/phenol/glycoside microstate is guessed, conversion changes the graph, or botanical occurrence is overstated. A hold applies to that ligand, not automatically to every other ligand. |
| **3. Freeze analysis before first candidate run** | Specify the receptor pair, search box and settings, fixed seed list, pocket-boundary rule, ligand-specific pose-cluster and clash checks, and a complete-results table including failures and nonconvergent poses. Include unchanged MIG as the receptor-specific positive pose control; no E32/E64 rerun is required when hashes match. | Box or chemistry is changed after seeing a score, only favorable seeds/poses are reported, or scores are treated as affinities. |
| **4. Interpret across states** | Before docking, fix the ligand heavy-atom **rigid-core map** (all heavy atoms for a sufficiently rigid ligand; a stated aglycone/ring core for a flexible glycoside). Within **each** receptor state, at least **4/5 rank-1 poses** must occupy the predefined MIG pocket and lie within **2.0 Å unfitted core RMSD** of that state's core-pose medoid, with no severe internal or receptor clash. Report whether the same contact pattern occurs across both states; retain contradictory and off-site poses. | Fewer than 4/5 per state meet the frozen cluster/pocket rule, a proposed contact relies on an unvalidated fixed-water bridge, or either state's result depends on one favorable seed. Such a result is **inconclusive**, not a negative biochemical result. The threshold is a prospective reproducibility screen, not proof of affinity. |

For an actual **production potency comparison**, these pilot gates are insufficient: obtain a validated glycan-aware receptor or an independently justified bound on glycan-omission effects, a defensible water/protonation treatment, and independent same-target biochemical positives/negatives before interpreting score order as a screening signal. If those requirements cannot be met, keep the work explicitly as receptor-model-sensitive pose hypotheses. The ten prepared [Vina ligand files](../../data/metadata/vina_ligand_preparation_results.csv) currently have preliminary PDBQT QC but all say `docking_microstate_review_pending`; none automatically passes Gate 2.
