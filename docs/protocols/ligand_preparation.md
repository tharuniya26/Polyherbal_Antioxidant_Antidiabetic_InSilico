# Ligand identity verification and preparation protocol for CCDC GOLD

**Protocol status:** prospective; metadata verified 11 October 2026. No 3D structures, GOLD inputs, docking runs, or ADMET results have been produced.  
**Source register:** [final preliminary ligand library](../../data/compounds/final_preliminary_ligand_library.csv) and [structure verification table](../../data/metadata/ligand_structure_verification.csv).

## Scope and evidence boundary

The library has **10 assigned reference chemicals** but **12 plant–compound associations**. Prepare a chemical structure once per `structure_id`; keep its association IDs attached as provenance. In particular, rutin (`LIG02`) serves both AM02 and SA01. The unresolved *Aegle marmelos* kaempferol-rutinoside-like peak (AM_KR01) has no assigned structure ID and remains **on hold**; do not silently substitute *Senna auriculata* nicotiflorin (`LIG10`). The [*Aegle*–*Senna* review](../../literature/aegle_senna_compound_evidence_review.md), [evidence corrections](../compound_evidence_corrections.md), and [selection protocol](final_ligand_selection.md) explain botanical evidence limits. Published occurrence in another extract does **not** establish presence in this project's original aqueous leaf extracts, nor does it establish affinity or biological activity of an individual ligand.

The [target candidate register](../../data/metadata/target_candidates.csv) lists human maltase-glucoamylase and pancreatic α-amylase structures. A definitive docking environment, receptor protonation, binding-site waters, and validation procedure remain to be selected. Therefore the instructions below describe **future ligand preparation**, with pH/microstate decisions recorded against the eventual receptor setup.

## 1. Official identity check

For each distinct `structure_id`, compare the library's CID and `isomeric_SMILES` against the official [PubChem Compound record](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/) and its [PUG REST property endpoint](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/docs/pug-rest). On 11 October 2026, all 10 CIDs resolved and all 10 stored isomeric SMILES matched PubChem's `SMILES` field exactly; the library's canonical strings matched `ConnectivitySMILES`. PubChem currently uses `SMILES` for the isomeric representation and `ConnectivitySMILES` for its connectivity-only representation; see its [glossary](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/docs/glossary). Formula, total formal charge, and defined/undefined stereo counts are recorded in the CSV. All **reference** structures have formal charge zero. This is a database-reference check, **not** an assignment of ionization in a binding site or proof that source chromatograms resolve every stereoisomer.

### Explicit identity conflicts and holds

- **BD01 / LIG03:** the water-infusion identification concerns mixed *Boerhavia diffusa* aerial parts. The separate leaf GC-MS signal is a **tri-TMS derivative** from a methanolic extract, not the underivatized parent. CID 689043 specifies *E*-caffeic acid, but the botanical records do not establish E/Z geometry for the project's leaf extract. Keep LIG03 as a reference identity and pause preparation for this study until the parent/isomer choice is justified. [Primary evidence assessment](../compound_evidence_corrections.md).
- **SR02 / LIG05:** CID 72276 is the PubChem (2R,3R) (−)-epicatechin structure. The hot-water leaf HPLC report supports an epicatechin-labelled peak but does not provide independent chiral confirmation. Do not call that peak the verified CID 72276 stereoisomer. Hold stereodefined preparation until source/standard characterization is reviewed; if several isomers remain credible, retain distinct, labelled alternatives for any future sensitivity analysis.
- **VN03 / LIG09:** the PubChem iridoid-glycoside reference is fully stereodefined, but the available publisher abstract does not expose the water-only quantitative chromatogram. Keep the literature-occurrence gate separate from chemical-structure verification. Do not present a methanol-first aqueous partition as direct water extraction.
- **AM_KR01:** the reported LC-MS/MS ions support a kaempferol-diglycoside-like assignment, not an exact 3-O linkage or sugar stereochemistry. It has no CID, SMILES, or preparation status in the 10-row verification table. No 3D ligand may be built under this association without stronger identity evidence.

## 2. Prospective, reproducible 3D preparation workflow

The steps below are a recipe for a later authorized preparation stage; they have **not** been run. Keep an immutable copy of the input library, official record URL/metadata response, chosen starting `isomeric_SMILES`, and any later generated coordinates. Give every derivative a distinct identifier such as `LIG01_neutral_t1_c01` or `LIG01_anion_t1_c01`; do not overwrite the parent record. Link derivatives back to the parent `structure_id` and its association IDs.

1. **Approve an identity.** Require the CID, formula, bond connectivity, stereochemical labels, and source caveats in the table above to agree. Distinguish a source-level occurrence hold from a valid PubChem chemical reference. AM_KR01 cannot advance. Release LIG03, LIG05, or LIG09 only after recording the evidence decision and any alternate isomer as a distinct structure.
2. **Define the docking chemical environment.** Document the intended receptor, binding-site model, pH assumption, and any experimentally justified ionization information. Do not use a single guessed pH as proof of a microstate. At minimum, explicitly consider neutral acid versus carboxylate for gallic acid and caffeic acid if LIG03 is later released. Evaluate any other plausible phenolic deprotonation or tautomer with a documented pKa/chemical rationale; if uncertainty affects donor/acceptor patterns, prepare separately labelled alternatives. Keep sugar stereochemistry, C-glycosidic linkage in mangiferin, and aglycone connectivity fixed. Do not alter the PubChem parent silently.
3. **Construct coordinates from the verified isomeric SMILES** using a versioned 3D builder or a documented PubChem 3D reference *at the later preparation stage*. Store the tool name/version, input string and CID, random seed, conformer method, parameters, and output file hash. A PubChem 3D conformer, if obtained later, is an initial geometry, not a prepared docking ligand. No coordinates are retrieved at this stage.
4. **Add explicit hydrogens after choosing the microstate.** Confirm valence, bond orders, formal charge, hydrogen count, and donor/acceptor groups in the resulting molecular graph. GOLD's [user guide](https://www.ccdc.cam.ac.uk/media/Documentation/GOLD_User_Guide_2020_1.pdf) states that it uses an all-atom ligand model and infers hydrogen-bonding roles from hydrogens and bond orders. Its scoring does not simply use imported formal or partial charges; correct ionization must be encoded chemically in the structure. If a carboxylate is selected, check its bond/atom typing in the target GOLD version.
5. **Generate and minimize conformers.** Use a documented conformer algorithm and fixed random seed, with enough diverse starting geometries for rotatable bonds and glycosides; record the number attempted, failures, pruning rule, and retained conformers. Minimize each with a named, versioned force field suitable for organic molecules, such as MMFF94 when parameters are available. Reject failed or visibly distorted optimizations. Do not compare force-field energies across different protonation states as a binding-affinity ranking. GOLD varies torsions but does not repair poor bond lengths/angles or change stereochemistry; provide reasonable starting geometries. See the [GOLD guide](https://www.ccdc.cam.ac.uk/media/Documentation/GOLD_User_Guide_2020_1.pdf).
6. **Inspect with Avogadro when useful.** Import a copy of the chosen structure, verify molecular graph and stereo labels against the reference, add/check hydrogens, inspect clashes, and run geometry optimization or a conformer search if supported by the installed build. Record the Avogadro version and selected force field. Its [force-field guide](https://avogadro.cc/docs/optimizing-geometry/force-fields.html) describes MMFF94/GAFF for organic molecules; its [conformer guide](https://avogadro.cc/docs/optimizing-geometry/conformers.html) describes search options and inspection. Interactive adjustment is a documented operation, not a replacement for a reproducible saved seed and tool settings. Verify stereochemistry again after export.
7. **Export GOLD-compatible input.** Prefer one explicitly named, three-dimensional ligand per SDF or MOL2 record. GOLD also accepts MOL/mmCIF/CCD, while its [current file-format guidance](https://support.ccdc.cam.ac.uk/support/solutions/articles/103000306413-which-file-formats-does-gold-support-) says MOL2 is needed if atom types are set manually. Avoid PDB for ligand interchange because bond orders are error-prone. Preserve explicit hydrogens, bond orders, connectivity, charges/microstate, atom names, and stereochemistry. Read the exported file back with an independent parser and in GOLD/Hermes before use; check that no atom is assigned a dummy `Du` type and no ligand is truncated.

### Flexible glycosides

Rutin (LIG02), mangiferin (LIG04), agnuside (LIG09, on hold), and nicotiflorin (LIG10) need extra attention to sugar stereocentres, glycosidic linkage, ring pucker, hydroxyl placement, and many rotatable bonds. Use several diverse minimized starting conformers rather than a single flattened drawing; deduplicate conformers by heavy-atom geometry within each microstate and retain provenance. Inspect ring chirality before and after minimization and format conversion. GOLD can explore torsions, but it does not invert fixed stereocentres; its guide notes that alternate stereoisomers must be separately generated if genuinely uncertain. Do not treat a failure to sample all glycoside conformers as evidence of poor binding. LIG10 is a **Senna** reserve; it is not a validated structure for AM_KR01.

## 3. Quality gates and reproducibility record

Before any prepared file is admitted to a later GOLD run, record and check:

| Gate | Required record or check |
| --- | --- |
| Provenance | `structure_id`, association IDs, compound name, PubChem CID and record retrieval date, source paper(s), occurrence tier, and any unresolved botanical/solvent limits. |
| Molecular graph | Input isomeric SMILES, formula, expected parent charge, chosen prepared microstate formula/charge, heavy-atom count, bond orders, isotope labels, atom mapping, and distinct state ID. |
| Stereo | Atom and double-bond stereo assignments before and after 3D generation, minimization, and export; a mismatch rejects the file. Source-level ambiguities stay visible even when the PubChem reference is fully defined. |
| Geometry | Explicit hydrogen count, finite 3D coordinates, reasonable bond lengths/angles, valid aromaticity and ring geometry, no severe intramolecular clashes, force-field parameter coverage, convergence/failure status, and conformer diversity. |
| GOLD import | Successful SDF/MOL2 read-back, correct ligand count and atom names, donor/acceptor roles and ionization, acceptable atom/bond typing, no `Du` warnings, no missing hydrogens or unexpected fragments. |
| Reproducibility | Tool and version, operating environment, command/settings, seed, pH/microstate rationale, force field, minimization criterion, conformer generation/pruning rules, input/output hashes, reviewer, and date. |

The later study should report each microstate and stereoisomer explicitly and distinguish parent PubChem metadata from any prepared derivative. A quality-pass ligand is a **technically prepared model**, not experimental confirmation of botanical occurrence and not evidence of antioxidant or antidiabetic action.
