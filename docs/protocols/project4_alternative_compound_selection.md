# Step 71 — prospective alternative compound selection

## Scope and decision rule

This is a literature and chemical-input selection record for three plants in the original **aqueous-leaf** five-plant study. It does not amend the [existing ligand library](../../data/compounds/final_preliminary_ligand_library.csv), assign a compound to an original extract, or reinterpret earlier docking. The full considered-candidate register, including reserves and exclusions, is [the machine-readable table](../../data/metadata/project4_alternative_compound_candidates.csv). Selection preceded any new docking and uses, in order: compound-specific primary leaf evidence and solvent match; unambiguous molecular identity; then feasibility of a separately quality-checked input. A prior favorable or unfavorable Vina pose is not a selection criterion.

**Evidence tiers:** A = named compound identified in a published water-only leaf extract; B = named compound identified in leaves using another solvent; C = species or derivative evidence that does not directly establish the listed parent in water-only leaves; unverified = insufficient accessible compound-specific evidence. These grades describe published *botanical occurrence*, not analytical confirmation in this project's extracts. `PASS` below means eligible for a **separate input-qualification step**, not approved for docking. No proposed candidate is approved for docking by this review alone.

### Source audit

**Aegle.** [Ahmad et al. (2021)](https://doi.org/10.3390/plants10122573) authenticated *A. marmelos* leaves and used **ethanol** for the extract that yielded a rutin peak matched to an HPLC-PDA standard. Its separate wording about “aqueous” in a total-phenolic section is not proof of water-only extraction of that peak. [A methanol-leaf LC-MS/MS report](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.toxrep.2018.09.005) and [a 1:1 ethanol-water leaf LC-MS/MS study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-018-33503-x) corroborate the botanical association; neither is a water-only extract. Rutin is a defined PubChem quercetin-3-O-rutinoside reference; an HPLC retention match alone does not independently resolve every sugar stereocentre. The same LIG02 structure is already associated with *Senna auriculata* and must be prepared once, with separate plant provenance. **Reserve/context:** gallic acid LIG01 has the same ethanol-leaf study and an already reported inconclusive pose screen; its inclusion here does not erase that result. The proposed Aegle kaempferol-rutinoside assignment remains **HOLD** because the exact glycoside identity has not been established; nicotiflorin must not be substituted merely because its reference is available.

**Vitex.** [Abidin et al. (2014)](https://doi.org/10.1016/S1995-7645(14)60248-0) analysed botanist-identified *V. negundo* leaves by HPLC using a luteolin standard after four **organic-solvent** extraction systems. This is compound-specific leaf support, not direct aqueous-leaf evidence. Existing LIG08 was assigned to *Senna*; assigning its *molecular structure* to a new Vitex association adds botanical provenance, not a new unique molecule or a result for Vitex. [Wu et al. (2024)](https://doi.org/10.3390/molecules29133133) report leaf isoorientin and chlorogenic acid from **80% aqueous methanol**, which is not water-only. Isoorientin is a conditional reserve pending C-glycoside stereo and input work; chlorogenic acid remains held until its caffeoylquinic positional isomer is source-matched. Agnuside LIG09 remains an existing conditional reserve: the [water-versus-solvent comparison abstract](https://doi.org/10.1007/s42250-022-00383-8) does not expose all water-specific chromatographic evidence, while the [independent leaf HPLC study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpha.2013.09.008) extracts with methanol *before* aqueous partitioning. Its earlier inconclusive docking is retained but was not used to choose luteolin. Negundoside is held for the same abstract-level water-specific evidence gap plus structure assessment.

**Boerhavia.** [Pereira et al. (2009)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.fct.2009.05.033) report voucher-identified *B. diffusa* leaves and roots separately; dried material was boiled in water for 20 minutes. The **leaf** HPLC-DAD profile assigns a quercetin aglycone peak. [Ferreres et al. (2005)](https://doi.org/10.1002/pca.869) used HPLC-PAD-ESI-MS/MS to profile multiple leaf samples, supporting the phenolic-profile context; sample-to-sample variation and analytical assignment strength matter. This is a published water-leaf quercetin report, **not** a measurement of quercetin in the original project. HPLC-DAD is less structurally definitive than isolation plus NMR; a source peak should not be mistaken for a source-specific protonation state. Quercetin is the same LIG06 chemical already assigned to *Senna* and already used in a prior pilot. Add an association, not a duplicate chemical; do not retroactively label the Senna docking experiment as a Boerhavia extract test. Quercetin-3-O-robinobioside is a water-leaf **reserve** but stays on HOLD pending exact linkage/stereo and reference-structure mapping. Existing trans-caffeic acid LIG03 stays on HOLD: [underivatized caffeic acid in boiled-water **mixed aerial parts**](https://doi.org/10.3390/antiox10122003) and a [methanolic-leaf caffeic-acid **tri-TMS derivative**](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jtcme.2019.02.002) do not establish underivatized E/trans caffeic acid in isolated aqueous leaves. The derivative and parent are distinct chemical entities; the source E/Z assignment is unresolved. Ferulic-acid parent inference from a di-TMS methanolic-leaf peak is likewise excluded.

## Chemical-input status and pH 6.5

The existing [ligand 3D results](../../data/metadata/ligand_preparation_results.csv) and [Meeko preparation results](../../data/metadata/vina_ligand_preparation_results.csv) record reference formulae, PubChem CIDs and source isomeric SMILES, SDF record counts, Meeko readback and hashes. Rutin is C27H30O16 (43 heavy atoms; 60 QC-passed conformers; 16 PDBQT torsions), luteolin is C15H10O6 (21 heavy atoms; 24 conformers; 5 torsions), and quercetin is C15H10O7 (22 heavy atoms; 24 conformers). The neutral reference PDBQTs passed preliminary graph and coordinate round trips in that audit. The official [rutin](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/5280805), [luteolin](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/5280445) and [quercetin](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/5280343) CID pages define *reference structures*, not occurrence in any plant. The current assessment reuses the previously PubChem-checked identifiers and local hashes; an independent live PubChem structure download was not used here.

At pH 6.5, all three neutral phenolic references are chemically defined; possible phenolate states must be treated as **explicit alternative hypotheses**, with deprotonation site and tautomer recorded before a new input is frozen. The [prior quercetin pilot plan](vina_3L4W_phytochemical_pilot_plan.md) discusses method-dependent first-acid-dissociation values and a model 4′-O monoanion. It does not establish a protein-bound microstate or generalize that choice to luteolin or rutin. Rutin's source-level sugar stereochemistry and flexible glycoside conformations need special attention. A stored MMFF conformer is an isolated reference geometry, not a bioactive conformation. Standard Vina scoring does not validate phenolate populations or metal electrostatics. **No ligand variant is prepared or changed in Step 71.**

## Prospective later pose-only protocol

For a **later, separately qualified** input set, retain both hash-verified dry/no-NAG 3L4W receptor hypotheses: Asp443−/Asp542−/His600-HID (`7a76e0fa3b7bf2197714a57a88c60d2f4eefedccb18636951f3f643473afadf3`) and Asp443−/Asp542H(OD2)/His600-HID (`220e3585558e4bc261e6e76ddb036738dea827543e1b135399506e95ea9f93fd`). Use the [enlarged-box native-MIG control](vina_3L4W_large_box_mig_validation.md), which recovered rank-1 MIG poses in 10/10 tested receptor/seed combinations, without repeating it. The frozen box is centred at **(45.38664286, 92.35157143, 34.82200000) Å**, size **30 × 24 × 24 Å**. Candidate compatibility must be checked independently, especially for rutin's flexible glycoside. Proposed unchanged search settings are Vina 1.2.7, `vina` scoring, exhaustiveness 64, 20 modes, energy range 5.0, minimum RMSD 1.0, one CPU and seeds 1101/2202/3303/4404/5505.

Retain the [original pilot's frozen geometry rules](vina_3L4W_phytochemical_pilot_plan.md): all ligand heavy atoms inside the box; core centroid within 6.0 Å of deposited MIG; at least half of rigid-core atoms within 4.0 Å of a MIG heavy atom; reject ligand–receptor heavy-atom distances <1.8 Å or van-der-Waals overlap >0.8 Å and nonbonded internal heavy-atom distances <1.6 Å; warn for any box-face margin <1 Å. For each **ligand microstate and receptor state separately**, at least 4/5 rank-1 poses must satisfy pocket and clash rules and fall within 2.0 Å **unfitted** rigid-core RMSD of that state's medoid. A new input-qualification protocol must freeze a chemically verified core/atom map and deterministic conformer/starting-pose allocation **before** any search; the existing mangiferin/quercetin maps cannot simply be copied to these molecules. Report all seeds and failures. The original-versus-enlarged pilot demonstrated that geometric conclusions can change with box size. Omitted N-linked glycans and crystallographic waters, provisional receptor and ligand protonation, rigid-receptor sampling and Vina score ranking remain limitations even when the geometry screen passes.

The next task is a read-only-plus-derived-input qualification for these three references: recheck source and PubChem graphs, SDF/PDBQT hashes, pH-6.5 microstates, conformer coverage, ligand-specific rigid cores and enlarged-box fit; then issue a frozen PASS/HOLD input manifest. No new docking, scoring, ADMET or molecular dynamics was performed here.
