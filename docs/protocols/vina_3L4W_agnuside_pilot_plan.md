## Scientific scope and existing controls

Neither source directly assigns all nine stereocentres to its leaf analyte. The original project aqueous leaves were **not** analytically shown to contain agnuside. The neutral PubChem structure is therefore an explicitly labelled reference hypothesis, and no alternative agnuside ionization or tautomer is introduced in this pilot.

## Deterministic conformer selection

The existing ETKDG/MMFF94s SDF has **60/60 chemically valid 3D records** and **40** approximately distinct full-heavy conformers at a 0.5 Å fitted, symmetry-aware RMSD greedy threshold. All records were rechecked for CID, isomeric graph, source metadata, complete explicit hydrogens, nine stereocentres inferred from 3D, finite coordinates, plausible bond lengths, severe internal heavy-atom contacts, and finite stored MMFF94s energy. The selected source record is a modelling starting structure, never a known protein-bound pose.

Selection was frozen **before docking** by this deterministic algorithm:

1. Restrict to records with stored MMFF94s energy **≤12.0 kcal/mol above the pool minimum** (18 eligible records). This is a broad same-molecule, same-neutral-state filter, not a thermodynamic population estimate.
2. Select the global minimum-energy record, breaking ties by the lowest one-based SDF record number.
3. For each remaining eligible record, calculate (a) best fitted **33-heavy-atom** RMSD with RDKit graph symmetry and (b) RMSD of flexible heavy atoms **10–33 after aligning only the nine-heavy-atom iridoid bicycle (SDF atoms 1–9)**, considering both graph-allowed automorphisms. Require minimum distances to **all previously selected** records of at least **1.5 Å full-heavy** and **2.0 Å flexible-region** RMSD.
4. Iteratively choose the candidate maximizing its minimum flexible-region RMSD to the selected set. Resolve ties by greater minimum full-heavy RMSD, then lower stored energy, then lower record number. Stop at **five** records, one per prespecified seed. The validator fails if the computed selection changes.

## Frozen pose analysis and failure handling

For **every returned mode**, use Meeko/RDKit to verify the CID 442416 reference graph, nine stereocentres, exact 33-heavy-atom map, PDBQT atom types/charges, finite coordinates, sensible heavy-bond geometry and internal nonbonded geometry. Preserve the original receptor coordinate frame. Define the rigid core as **one-based source SDF heavy atoms 1–9**, translated to each PDBQT through its recorded index map. The two graph-allowed automorphisms may be used only where they preserve chemical atom equivalence; do not independently fit returned poses to the MIG crystal reference or to one another when computing pose-cluster RMSD.

Apply the [existing frozen pilot rule](vina_3L4W_phytochemical_pilot_plan.md) **separately within each receptor state**. A rank-1 pose passes the MIG-pocket screen only if all ligand heavy atoms are inside the box, its nine-atom core centroid is **≤6 Å** from the deposited MIG heavy centroid, and **≥50%** of core atoms are within **4 Å** of a deposited MIG heavy atom. Flag a severe clash if any receptor–ligand heavy pair is **<1.8 Å** or overlaps by **>0.8 Å** based on RDKit van der Waals radii, or if a nonbonded intraligand heavy pair at least four graph bonds apart is **<1.6 Å**. Identify the rank-1 medoid within each receptor state as the observed rank-1 core pose minimizing summed pairwise **unfitted, symmetry-aware** RMSD across the five seeds, ties broken by smaller seed. At least **4/5 rank-1 poses** must pass pocket, no-severe-clash and **≤2.0 Å core RMSD to that medoid** for that receptor state's geometric PASS. Classify a completed but subthreshold condition **INCONCLUSIVE**; invalid chemistry, missing required input or unresolved technical failure is **BLOCKED**. Report each seed and all returned modes regardless of classification. A geometric PASS is not evidence of biochemical binding or inhibition.

For every mode also record minimum distance to each of the six box faces and flag any heavy atom **<1.0 Å** from a face. As a separate, **read-only diagnostic**, compare the pose with water oxygens in the original deposited 3L4W PDB, recording the nearest water ID/distance and any heavy-atom approach **<1.8 Å**. This diagnostic is motivated by the [gallic-acid dry-pocket artifact investigation](vina_3L4W_gallic_acid_off_pocket_investigation.md); it **does not change** the frozen geometric PASS rule and static waters must not be called validated dynamic solvent. Report catalytic-residue heavy-atom distances as proximity only, not assigned hydrogen bonds.

If a Vina invocation fails, times out or reports insufficient memory, retain its attempt log and partial output. A technical retry may reduce *concurrent process count only* while keeping the same hashed inputs, seed, box and command settings; save it as a separate attempt. Do not equate a parseable pose output with a confirmed zero process exit. If a returned mode cannot pass molecule reconstruction or atom-map QC, record it as invalid with the exact error. Do not discard a nonconvergent seed, off-pocket mode or box-edge pose. Do not raise exhaustiveness or alter any model after viewing pilot scores.
