The caffeic-acid carboxylic group has a reported aqueous first pKa around 4–5; a [primary spectrophotometric study](https://doiserbia.nb.rs/Article.aspx?id=0352-51391800116D) reports approximately 4.02 for its first dissociation in water and a substantially higher second dissociation. Thus at pH 6.5 the **carboxylate is a justified bulk-solution hypothesis**, while the neutral acid is retained as a labelled sensitivity reference. The reported pKa is not a protein-pocket measurement. Phenolate variants and other proton-transfer/tautomer alternatives are **HOLD** without site-specific evidence. For agnuside, no experimentally verified pKa or source-specific alternative tautomer was established here; the neutral PubChem reference is the sole PASS chemical input. Generating a deprotonated agnuside from an assumed phenolic pKa would be unjustified. The lowest stored isolated MMFF conformer is an input representative, not a proven bound conformation.

## 3L4W compatibility and future gates

The validator confirmed both unchanged dry/no-NAG receptor PDBQT hashes: Asp443−/Asp542−/His600-HID `7a76e0fa3b7bf2197714a57a88c60d2f4eefedccb18636951f3f643473afadf3` and Asp443−/Asp542H(OD2)/His600-HID `220e3585558e4bc261e6e76ddb036738dea827543e1b135399506e95ea9f93fd`. It rechecked the original neutral-MIG reference and the **10/10** enlarged-box rank-1 native-MIG pose recoveries, including their saved result, input, log and output hashes, without rerunning them. Future exploratory work would use the independently established **30 × 24 × 24 Å** box centered at `(45.38664286, 92.35157143, 34.82200000)` Å, Vina 1.2.7, `vina` scoring, exhaustiveness 64, 20 modes, energy range 5.0, minimum RMSD 1.0, one CPU and seeds 1101, 2202, 3303, 4404 and 5505. This is a **planned setup**, not evidence that either candidate has been docked or that Vina can score all omitted solvent chemistry.

The LIG03 selected source conformer spans about **8.70 × 3.10 × 2.42 Å**, and its largest source-conformer heavy-centroid radius is **4.758 Å**; if centered, its conservative clearance to the narrowest box face is **7.242 Å**. LIG09's selected conformer spans about **11.90 × 7.59 × 6.50 Å**; its largest source-conformer radius is **9.461 Å**, leaving only **2.539 Å** of conservative centered clearance for that most extended conformer. Thus both chemical inputs fit a centered 30 × 24 × 24 Å box, but agnuside needs an explicit box-face analysis on **every** future pose and diversified, preselected starting conformers. A centered fit does not guarantee that Vina's optimized poses avoid a face.

For any separately approved pose-only experiment, the manifest freezes a nine-heavy-atom scaffold map: **SDF atoms 1–9** for LIG03 (aromatic ring and conjugated E side-chain carbons) and **SDF atoms 1–9** for LIG09 (fused iridoid bicyclic scaffold; glucose and the flexible benzoyl appendage remain outside the core). The manifest records each SDF-to-PDBQT atom map and graph-allowed automorphism count. The previously frozen MIG-pocket rule remains: all ligand heavy atoms inside the box, core centroid ≤6 Å from deposited MIG, and ≥50% of core atoms within 4 Å of a deposited MIG heavy atom. Retain the severe receptor-clash rule (<1.8 Å heavy pair or >0.8 Å van der Waals overlap), internal clash rule (<1.6 Å for graph-separated heavy atoms), and **unfitted, symmetry-aware** core-RMSD clustering ≤2 Å to the within-pair rank-1 medoid in at least **4/5** prespecified seeds. Report every failed, off-pocket or face-adjacent pose. In light of the [gallic-acid off-pocket water conflict](vina_3L4W_gallic_acid_off_pocket_investigation.md), any rank-1 pose should also be compared *afterward* to the unchanged deposited 3L4W waters; this is a diagnostic flag, not a change to the frozen PASS rule or a validated static-water score.
