## Research scope and botanical evidence

The [preliminary ligand library](../../data/compounds/final_preliminary_ligand_library.csv) identifies mangiferin as LIG04/PubChem [CID 5281647](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/5281647)/SR01 and quercetin as LIG06/[CID 5280343](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/5280343)/SA02. [Yoshino et al.](https://doi.org/10.1271/bbb.80854) reported compound-specific HPLC measurement of mangiferin in a water extract of *Salacia reticulata* leaves (Tier A botanical occurrence); that assay did not independently resolve every glycoside stereocentre. [Nakamura et al.](https://doi.org/10.1248/cpb.c14-00420) isolated quercetin from *Senna auriculata* (syn. *Cassia auriculata*) leaves extracted with **80% aqueous acetone**, then fractionated with ethyl acetate (Tier B; leaf occurrence, other extraction). **Neither molecule was analytically demonstrated in the original project's aqueous extracts.** Neither literature occurrence nor the current input qualification predicts α-glucosidase inhibition.

## Input audit and chemical decision

The validator checked **all** 60 LIG04 and 24 LIG06 SDF records against the frozen CID-backed isomeric graph, CID and source metadata; explicit hydrogens; finite 3D coordinates and bond lengths; 3D-implied stereochemistry; recorded MMFF94s energies; selected-record identity; and PDBQT-to-RDKit graph/stereochemistry readback. One-based source SDF **record 1** is the recorded minimum isolated-molecule MMFF94s conformer in both pools. Its energy is **120.47779257 kcal/mol** for LIG04 and **43.38272614 kcal/mol** for LIG06. The source pools have **13/60** and **4/24** approximately distinct full-heavy conformers at a 0.5 Å fitted-RMSD greedy threshold, respectively. These numbers describe the existing ETKDG/MMFF94s pools, not proven bound-conformer coverage or absolute thermodynamics; glycoside flexibility is especially consequential for mangiferin. The existing Meeko neutral PDBQTs retain 30 and 22 heavy atoms, five and zero specified stereocentres, formal charge zero, and 10 and 6 reported torsions. Their heavy coordinates differ from the selected SDFs only by PDBQT rounding (<0.0011 Å). The complete one-based **heavy-atom** SDF-to-PDBQT index maps and file SHA256s are in the manifest. Hydrogen indices are not used as reference atom maps because removal of one phenolic H renumbers subsequent hydrogens.

The [aqueous mangiferin acid-constant study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.saa.2005.09.009) assigns its first dissociation to 6-OH with pKₐ **6.52 ± 0.06**. At preparation pH **6.5**, neutral and first-anion forms are both plausible *in that aqueous study's conditions*; the protein environment can shift this equilibrium. A separate [mixed-solvent mangiferin study](https://doi.org/10.1039/C6RA06328D) reports a different value in DMSO/water and is not treated as the aqueous pKₐ. For quercetin, an [aqueous spectroscopic study](https://doi.org/10.1002/chem.202001956) obtained a first pKₐ near **7.3** but also summarized substantial method-dependent spread (**5.50–7.65**). The 4′-O monoanion is a chemically plausible model supported by [computational acid–base work](https://doi.org/10.1039/D0RA09632F); the available measurements do not fix its relative population versus other monoanion regioisomers in this receptor. Neither PDBQT is declared a measured bound-state microstate. Only the parent keto skeleton and these single-deprotonation variants are frozen here; further tautomer selection requires independent justification before adding any model. The variant preparation removes one specified phenolic H, preserves every parent heavy-atom coordinate and stereocentre, and lets Meeko assign new Gasteiger charges. No neutral file was replaced and no energy-minimized anion conformer is asserted. Standard Vina's [FAQ](https://autodock-vina.readthedocs.io/en/stable/faq.html) states that its scoring function ignores supplied partial charges; therefore, a neutral/phenolate score comparison cannot establish the correct electrostatic microstate or proton-coupled binding energetics.

## Prospective Vina setup and result rules

The box is the earlier **MIG-derived crystallographic** box: center **(45.38664286, 92.35157143, 34.82200000) Å**, dimensions **24 × 18 × 18 Å**. Freeze **AutoDock Vina 1.2.7**, `--scoring vina`, `--exhaustiveness 64`, `--num_modes 20`, `--energy_range 5.0`, `--min_rmsd 1.0`, `--cpu 1`, and independent seeds **1101, 2202, 3303, 4404, 5505**. The validated Vina executable SHA256 is in the manifest. All four ligand states are predeclared against both receptor states: **40 possible diagnostic runs**, with independent logs, commands, start structures, runtime, outputs and output hashes. Create reproducible noncrystal randomized starts, using one documented ligand-specific procedure and the same seed schedule for both receptor states. Source conformer 1 is only the initial chemical geometry; Vina must still explore orientation and allowed torsions. Keep any future additional source conformer test separate and prespecified. Do not change chemistry, box or seeds after inspecting scores.

The rigid-core atom maps, one-based in the source SDF and translated to PDBQT serials by the Meeko map, are frozen in the CSV. **LIG04:** xanthone fused-ring skeleton plus carbonyl oxygen, 15 heavy atoms. **LIG06:** fused chromone skeleton plus carbonyl oxygen, 11 heavy atoms; its rotatable pendant phenyl ring is outside this core. Use the same core identities across neutral/anion forms. For each returned pose, reconstruct/verify all ligand heavy atoms and graph; preserve original receptor-frame coordinates. Compute pairwise **unfitted** core RMSD from the mapped atoms in the unchanged receptor frame, allowing only graph-proven equivalent-atom permutations. Do not align each pose to another pose to reduce RMSD. The medoid is the observed rank-1 core pose minimizing the sum of pairwise core RMSDs among the five prespecified seeds for that *ligand state and receptor state*; break ties by smaller seed. Report full-heavy and core placement separately.

**Pocket membership:** a rank-1 pose passes when all ligand heavy atoms lie inside the frozen box, its rigid-core centroid is at most **6.0 Å** from the deposited MIG heavy-atom centroid, and at least **50% of rigid-core atoms** are within **4.0 Å** of at least one deposited MIG heavy atom (crystal coordinates used only as a pocket reference). This rule checks competitive-site localization, not reproduction of MIG's chemistry. **Severe clash:** fail a pose if any ligand–receptor heavy-atom pair is <**1.8 Å** or has >**0.8 Å** van-der-Waals radius overlap; also fail if a nonbonded ligand heavy-atom pair separated by at least four graph bonds is <**1.6 Å**. Use RDKit van-der-Waals radii, ignoring hydrogens and reporting any atom-typing exception explicitly. These are prespecified geometry screens, not energy corrections. Include a size/box-edge warning if any heavy atom is within 1.0 Å of a face even when technically inside.

**Convergence pass:** within **each receptor state**, at least **4/5 rank-1 poses** for the same ligand *and microstate* must independently pass pocket membership, avoid severe clashes, and lie within **2.0 Å unfitted core RMSD** of that state's medoid. Report all five rank-1 poses, all returned modes and failures; also report whether the two receptor states yield comparable core placement and catalytic contact patterns. Evaluate neutral and monoanion separately; do not select the more favorable microstate or receptor by Vina score. Failure of this prospective screen is **nonconvergence/inconclusive pose placement**, not evidence that a compound lacks biochemical activity. A score ordering, even when poses cluster, does not establish binding affinity, inhibition constant, synergy or clinical effect. The omitted glycans, waters and alternate protonation states remain unresolved if the screen passes.
