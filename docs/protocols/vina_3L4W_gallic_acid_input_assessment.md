# Step 62 — gallic acid input qualification for the 3L4W pose-only pilot

**Decision (15 October 2026):** **PASS** the neutral gallic-acid reference as an explicitly labelled minority-solution sensitivity hypothesis, and **PASS** a separately prepared gallic-acid carboxylate as the primary bulk-water pH 6.5 hypothesis. Both pass *input-quality* checks against the same two frozen 3L4W receptor microstates. These decisions do not establish the bound-state protonation, gallic acid binding, or presence in the project's original aqueous leaf extract. No gallic-acid docking or scoring was run. The four input-pair records are in the [machine-readable manifest](../../data/metadata/vina_3L4W_gallic_acid_input_manifest.csv); the [validator](../../scripts/validate_vina_3L4W_gallic_acid_inputs.py) recreates and checks only these inputs.

## Chemical identity and evidence boundary

[PubChem CID 370](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/370) identifies gallic acid as 3,4,5-trihydroxybenzoic acid, neutral formula **C7H6O5**, zero formal charge and no stereocentres. Its source isomeric SMILES, `C1=C(C=C(C(=C1O)O)O)C(=O)O`, canonicalizes to the same graph as the project LIG01 source SDF and the [preliminary library](../../data/compounds/final_preliminary_ligand_library.csv). The explicitly deprotonated **carboxylate**, `O=C([O-])c1cc(O)c(O)c(O)c1`, has formula **C7H5O5−** and charge **−1**. The charged form is a modelling hypothesis derived from CID 370; it is not a separate claim that PubChem deposited that specific docked microstate.

The AM01 *Aegle marmelos* association is **Tier B**. The [leaf study](https://doi.org/10.3390/plants10122573) extracted leaves with **ethanol at 40 °C for 3 h** and assigned gallic acid by HPLC-PDA retention-time comparison with a standard. Retention-time matching is less specific than orthogonal structural confirmation. Its total phenolic content in gallic-acid equivalents is an assay calibration, not an independent measurement of gallic acid. This paper does **not** identify gallic acid in the original Project 4 **aqueous leaf extract**. No concentration in any formulation is known.

An [experimental potentiometric study](https://doi.org/10.1080/00032718508068758) reports gallic-acid first dissociation pK approximately **4.4** and a later dissociation near **8.6** under its stated measurement conditions. The first ionization is the carboxylic acid; thus carboxylate is strongly favored over neutral acid in bulk aqueous solution at preparation pH **6.5**. This does not determine the enzyme-bound microstate. Phenolic ionization, tautomer/proton transfer and local electrostatics are not resolved by the present evidence; no phenolate or dianion was prepared. The neutral acid remains a defined reference/sensitivity model and must not be described as the dominant pH 6.5 solution form. No variant was chosen by a docking score.

The validator checked **24/24** original SDF records for CID, source SMILES, graph, formula, charge, explicit hydrogens, finite three-dimensional coordinates, bond lengths and absence of stereocentres. All were previously ETKDGv3 generated and MMFF94s minimized. The recorded minimum-energy neutral conformer is **SDF record 1, source conformer 19**, with stored isolated-molecule MMFF94s energy **−12.22774638 kcal/mol**; the pool spans **−12.22774638 to −2.34616522 kcal/mol**. A greedy 0.5 Å *fitted* full-heavy comparison yields one distinguishable heavy conformer in this small, relatively rigid pool. This describes the sampled isolated molecules, not the bound conformation or an exhaustive proton/phenol orientation search. The selected neutral heavy-atom span is **6.1411 × 4.6190 × 1.6212 Å**.

The existing neutral PDBQT reconstructs as the exact neutral heavy-atom graph with charge sum **0.000**, **12 heavy atoms**, **5 torsions**, and maximum heavy-coordinate difference **0.0006 Å** from its selected SDF record. To construct the carboxylate, the script removed the hydrogen on source SDF **atom 12** (the carboxyl OH oxygen), set that oxygen to −1, retained the heavy-atom index order, and minimized the resulting isolated anion with supported **MMFF94s** parameters. The minimization converged at **13.43245315 kcal/mol** on its own force-field surface; this value must **not** be compared with the neutral MMFF energy as a solution or binding free-energy difference. The minimized carboxyl C–O distances are **1.2722 and 1.2695 Å**. The new SDF readback retains the intended graph, −1 charge and finite 3D geometry. Meeko 0.8.0 with Gasteiger charges yields a PDBQT readback of the same graph, charge sum **−1.000**, **12 heavy atoms**, **4 torsions** and maximum SDF-to-PDBQT heavy-coordinate shift **0.0006 Å**. The original neutral SDF/PDBQT were not changed.

For both states, one-based SDF **rigid-core heavy indices 1–10** are frozen: six aromatic carbons, three directly attached phenolic oxygens and the carboxyl carbon. Carboxyl oxygens 11–12 are excluded from the core because their orientation and resonance representation are state sensitive, but remain in every full-molecule identity, box and clash check. The complete one-based SDF-to-PDBQT heavy-atom maps and state-specific core serials are in the manifest. The heavy graph has **two** atom automorphisms, so a future core-cluster calculation must consider the chemically equivalent ring mapping and report the minimum **unfitted** crystal-frame RMSD. The existing core rule is a geometric screen, not a claim that the carboxylate is crystallographically observed.
