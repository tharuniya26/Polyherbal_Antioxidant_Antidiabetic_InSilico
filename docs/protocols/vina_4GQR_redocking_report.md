# 4GQR/MYC diagnostic native-pose redocking with AutoDock Vina

**Status:** five prespecified Vina 1.2.7 runs completed on 13 October 2026. **The predefined rank-1 heavy-atom RMSD screen was not passed (0/5 at ≤2.0 Å).** This is a diagnostic test of one explicitly labelled dry, no-NAG local-pocket model of human pancreatic α-amylase, not validation of binding energies, inhibition or an antidiabetic effect. No phytochemical library docking or ADMET was performed.

## Structural and chemical preflight

The starting structure is the deposited [4GQR human pancreatic α-amylase–myricetin complex](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/4GQR), determined by X-ray diffraction at 1.20 Å. The [primary structural study](https://doi.org/10.1021/jm301273u) places MYC in the catalytic site. The deposited [MYC chemical definition](https://www.rcsb.org/ligand/MYC) and the unchanged PDB/mmCIF/CCD files were compared with the frozen SHA256 values in the [preparation audit](../../data/metadata/vina_alternative_receptor_preparation_results.csv). The [script](../../scripts/run_vina_4GQR_redocking.py) refuses altered sources. Its [experiment manifest](../../docking/validation/vina/4GQR_diagnostic_redocking/experiment_manifest.json) records the verified source, software and derived receptor hashes.

The native-pose [SDF](../../docking/validation/vina/4GQR_MYC_native_pose.sdf) contains one neutral myricetin reference, formula **C15H10O8**, with all **23 deposited heavy atoms**, a complete CCD-backed graph and no stereogenic atom assignment. Its heavy-atom names and coordinates match deposited MYC A504. The [native PDBQT](../../docking/validation/vina/4GQR_MYC_crystal_pose_reference.pdbqt) has **29 explicit atoms and seven Meeko torsions**; Meeko 0.8.0/RDKit readback preserved graph and heavy-atom coordinates. The deposited [atom map](../../docking/validation/vina/4GQR_MYC_atom_map.csv) and [SDF-to-PDBQT bridge](../../docking/validation/vina/4GQR_MYC_sdf_to_pdbqt_atom_map.csv) are used for every pose. The original crystal SDF and PDBQT were kept unchanged and used only as references, never as sole Vina search starts. Neutral phenol protonation/tautomer choice at pH 7.5 is a preparation hypothesis, not an X-ray assignment.

The [official MYC ligand-validation page](https://www.rcsb.org/ligand-validation/4gqr/MYC) reports all heavy atoms modeled but **0.40 occupancy**, real-space correlation **0.662**, and ligand bond/angle geometry outliers. Thus even an exact computational recovery would be relative to a partly occupied, geometrically imperfect deposited pose. It does not erase the structure's published active-site interpretation.

The existing [preliminary receptor PDBQT](../../structures/proteins/vina_prepared/4GQR_validation_local_noNAG_Meeko0.8.0.pdbqt) had a verified PCA A1 terminal template, **Ca A502** and **Cl A503**, provisional Asp197⁻/Glu233⁻/Asp300⁻/His201-HID microstates at preparation pH **7.5**, and 496/496 modeled protein residues without missing standard side-chain atoms. One covalently linked NAG A501 was previously omitted **only** from this local model; its nearest deposited atom is about **50.93 Å** from MYC. The ion PDBQT fields are `Ca +2.000` and `Cl −1.000`; nearest deposited Ca and Cl are about **10.14** and **8.49 Å** from MYC, respectively. They were **retained**, including PCA. Their biological/structural roles are not negated by their distance to this ligand. Standard Vina's [scoring documentation](https://autodock-vina.readthedocs.io/en/stable/faq.html) says supplied partial charges are not used as a full electrostatic model; successful input parsing cannot validate Ca coordination or Cl electrostatics.

The preliminary receptor retained eight waters (HOH A905, A1083, A1101, A1142, A1144, A1177, A1204, A1244) with modeled O–H distances **1.176–1.190 Å**. Three deposited water oxygens are within 2.63–2.87 Å of MYC, but their modeled hydrogen directions are not established by diffraction. In the preliminary PDBQT, an MYC hydrogen–HOH A1204 hydrogen separation is **0.907 Å**. It would be chemically unsound to score that fixed-water configuration as a validated solvation model. The script therefore created a **new, explicitly dry** [receptor PDBQT](../../structures/proteins/vina_prepared/4GQR_dry_noNAG_CA_CL_PCA_pH7p5_diagnostic_Meeko0.8.0.pdbqt) by removing exactly the 24 PDBQT atoms of those eight HOH residues from the audited preliminary variant. All **4,840 other atom lines, coordinates, types and charge fields** are preserved; Ca, Cl and PCA remain. The dry PDBQT SHA256 is `71bee218c2cfede049ad8254bc39b33cb7c916055c0355e17236a2227a3e52b4`. This is a transparent local-pocket sensitivity choice, **not** proof that these waters are absent in solution. The deposited MYC has no protein-heavy-atom contact below 2.0 Å (closest approximately 2.403 Å to Asp197 OD2); protein/ligand hydrogen placement still needs a chemically optimized joint assessment.

**Pre-run decision:** the source, ligand graph/map and local receptor passed the checks required for a *diagnostic pose-recovery search* with a labeled dry model. The model was **not cleared for physically interpretable ion-dependent scoring or full biological validation**. It retains the linked-glycan omission, provisional catalytic and ligand protonation, missing explicit solvent, and partial-occupancy reference uncertainty.

## Search setup and pose analysis

The box center is the centroid of the **deposited MYC heavy coordinates**, **(13.44317391, 15.13530435, 39.43613043) Å**. The box is **26 × 24 × 28 Å**. Each half-extent was rounded up to include at least **5 Å** beyond MYC and the Asp197, Glu233, Asp300 and His201 catalytic side-chain heavy atoms. This was fixed before inspecting any docking score.

For seeds **1101, 2202, 3303, 4404, 5505**, NumPy PCG64 generated an independent rigid quaternion rotation plus translation of the intact neutral MYC PDBQT. The five starting heavy-atom RMSDs from the crystal were **6.8072, 7.1447, 6.7141, 7.6025 and 6.9095 Å**. Graph, bond lengths, atom map, charge and original reference pose stayed unchanged. Vina then ran its own seeded global search with `--scoring vina --exhaustiveness 32 --num_modes 20 --energy_range 5.0 --min_rmsd 1.0 --cpu 1`. All five runs used the same receptor, ligand microstate and box. Exact commands, settings, timestamps, hashes, status and runtime are in the [results CSV](../../data/metadata/vina_4GQR_redocking_results.csv) and individual [logs and poses](../../docking/validation/vina/4GQR_diagnostic_redocking/).

Every returned PDBQT was read back through Meeko and checked against the neutral MYC graph, the fixed 23-heavy-atom bridge and original bond geometry. The script used both chemically allowed MYC graph automorphisms to calculate the **minimum symmetry-aware heavy-atom RMSD** in the common crystal coordinate frame. No pose was independently fitted or superposed to MYC. Vina's inter-mode RMSD was not substituted for this crystal-reference RMSD. The predefined screening criterion was **rank-1 RMSD ≤2.0 Å**; best-of-all-returned-modes RMSD is reported separately.

The rank-1 poses cluster at **2.4926–2.5601 Å** from the deposited MYC reference (mean **2.5287 Å**) and at **0.0070–0.1280 Å** from one another after symmetry mapping in the common receptor frame. Thus the search repeatedly favors a similar pose, but it is **outside the prespecified 2 Å recovery screen in all five runs**. Only seed 5505 sampled a pose below 2 Å, and Vina ranked it **17th**, with a score **1.238** units less favorable than rank 1. The closest poses in seeds 1101, 2202, 3303 and 4404 remained above 2 Å. This is a clear native-pose recovery and score-ordering limitation under this setup; it must not be represented as a successful validation because one lower-ranked pose crossed the screen.

All run outputs, including alternative modes, were retained. The [100-pose table](../../docking/validation/vina/4GQR_diagnostic_redocking/all_pose_metrics.csv) and per-seed pose tables support independent review. Seed 5505's recorded wall time was unusually long because the host appears to have been idle/suspended during the run; the five recorded times are preserved but are **not suitable as comparative search-efficiency measurements**.

## Interpretation and next validation gate

The initial 4GQR/MYC dry local model **fails the predefined rank-1 ≤2 Å pose-recovery criterion (0/5)**; one of five runs has *any* returned pose below 2 Å. The tight rank-1 cluster suggests the main outcome is reproducible at E32, while the single rank-17 near-native pose leaves search coverage and ranking as separate open questions. A controlled **E64 five-seed search with the exact same chemistry, box, reference and mapping** is a justified next diagnostic convergence test. It should be specified and reported as a new experiment, including every failed run; changing waters, protonation, ions or box simultaneously would not isolate search exhaustiveness. E128 would require a further stated reason after E64.

Even improved pose recovery would remain conditional: the deposited MYC has partial occupancy and geometry outliers; the dry model omits binding-site waters and a distant linked NAG; catalytic and MYC protonation are provisional; and standard Vina cannot validate calcium/chloride physics from the PDBQT charge fields. A separate water strategy and ion-aware or otherwise independently validated scoring approach are needed before interpreting score differences or ranking phytochemicals. 4GQR does **not** advance to production phytochemical docking on this result. The previous 3L4W data and all original 4GQR files were unchanged.
