# 4GQR/MYC Glu233H redocking sensitivity test

**Step 55, 13 October 2026.** A single, prespecified Glu233 protonation change was tested against the failed 4GQR/myricetin (MYC) native-pose benchmark. The new dry, no-NAG receptor with Glu233H on OE2 passed input and chemical quality checks. AutoDock Vina 1.2.7 completed **5/5** exhaustiveness-64 runs. **The predefined rank-1 heavy-atom RMSD ≤2.0 Å screen failed again (0/5).** No production phytochemical docking or ADMET was performed.

The [Step 54 investigation](vina_4GQR_failure_investigation.md) identified Glu233H as one conditional microstate hypothesis after PROPKA 3.5.1 predicted Glu233 pKa **8.73** from deposited 4GQR and **9.29** from the local prepared structure at the crystal-growth pH **7.5**. These are computational estimates, not measured protonation states. The original [E32 experiment](vina_4GQR_redocking_report.md) and [E64 convergence experiment](vina_4GQR_convergence_assessment.md) each failed the same rank-1 screen at 0/5; their files were read but not changed or rerun.

## Frozen inputs and one-variable receptor preparation

The E64 receptor was the dry, no-NAG, Ca/Cl/PCA-retaining 4GQR PDBQT (SHA256 `71bee218c2cfede049ad8254bc39b33cb7c916055c0355e17236a2227a3e52b4`). Its source prepared PDB retained the eight site/ion-shell waters later removed for the E64 dry model. The new [Glu233H PDB](../../docking/validation/vina/4GQR_Glu233H_sensitivity/4GQR_dry_noNAG_Glu233H_OE2_pH7p5.pdb) removes exactly those eight waters and adds only Glu233 HE2. Every other existing PDB atom line, including all protein heavy atoms and hydrogens, Ca A502, Cl A503, and terminal PCA A1, was preserved byte for byte. The distant linked NAG A501 remained omitted as in E64. The new [receptor PDBQT](../../docking/validation/vina/4GQR_Glu233H_sensitivity/4GQR_dry_noNAG_Glu233H_OE2_pH7p5_Meeko0.8.0.pdbqt) has SHA256 `8f85706775f8750f4689b4c6e33b28623a302d40ebaaef278838e9541e1c5fd3`.

The deposited Glu233 CD–OE1 and CD–OE2 distances are **1.254** and **1.251 Å**. The installed Meeko 0.8.0 `GLH` residue template (SHA256 `535dc75a2cc5db579a3114090c9ab1273892c556cb7cc1a850ce2c4cd57c7cde`) explicitly puts HE2 on **OE2**. The new HE2 was placed at **0.98 Å** from OE2 and a **108° CD–OE2–HE2** angle in the carboxyl plane, in the syn orientation toward OE1. This is a chemically plausible acid geometry chosen before any docking result; it is not an experimentally located hydrogen. The closest other receptor atom to this hydrogen is Ile235 HD13 at **3.150 Å**; its closest atom in the unchanged crystal MYC SDF is **3.231 Å**. No close hydrogen clash was introduced by this placement. Nearby deposited OE1 contacts include Ala198 backbone N at **2.800 Å** and HOH A905 O at **3.114 Å**; these distances do not establish hydrogen-bond direction. The receptor remains intentionally dry, so the water is contextual crystallographic evidence rather than a docked atom.

The script invoked `mk_prepare_receptor` with `--read_pdb`, `--write_pdbqt`, the previously validated PCA template (`--add_templates`), and `--set_template A:233=GLH`. The resulting PDBQT has **4,841** atoms: exactly the 4,840 baseline atoms plus Glu233 HE2 typed `HD`; both Glu233 oxygens remain typed `OA`. All other receptor PDBQT atom identities, coordinates, types, and partial-charge fields matched E64 within the stated **0.0011 Å / 0.0011 charge-unit** audit tolerance. Glu233's summed PDBQT partial charges changed from approximately **−1.000** to **0.000**, the expected residue-level bookkeeping for the protonation; Ca remained `Ca/+2.000`, Cl `Cl/−1.000`, and PCA was retained. The GLH template encodes the residue connectivity. Vina reached a deliberate invalid-grid guard after parsing the new receptor and the unchanged neutral MYC input, establishing input-format acceptance **without scoring**. PDBQT acceptance does not validate ion physics.

The preparation [manifest](../../docking/validation/vina/4GQR_Glu233H_sensitivity/experiment_manifest.json) records source/template hashes, software versions, geometry, charges, settings, and the preparation command. The [reproducibility script](../../scripts/run_vina_4GQR_glu233_sensitivity.py) supports `--prepare-only` and refuses to overwrite an existing artifact whose bytes differ.

## Controlled redocking and pose quality checks

The neutral [MYC PDBQT](../../docking/validation/vina/4GQR_MYC_crystal_pose_reference.pdbqt) and unchanged [crystal-reference SDF](../../docking/validation/vina/4GQR_MYC_native_pose.sdf) retained their audited hashes. The ligand has **23 heavy atoms**, complete CCD-backed connectivity, no assigned stereocentre, and two chemically allowed heavy-atom graph automorphisms. The same bijective [deposited atom map](../../docking/validation/vina/4GQR_MYC_atom_map.csv) and [PDBQT bridge](../../docking/validation/vina/4GQR_MYC_sdf_to_pdbqt_atom_map.csv) were reused. Each output was reconstructed with Meeko and checked against the reference molecular graph, atom identities/types, bond geometry, finite coordinates, and score ordering. RMSD is the minimum over the two automorphisms in the **unchanged crystal coordinate frame**, with no independent ligand fitting.

For each seed, the randomized ligand-start bytes were reproduced exactly from the frozen E64 start. All five starts were **6.71–7.60 Å** from the native pose, so the crystal reference was not used as the sole starting pose. The box remained centered at **(13.44317391, 15.13530435, 39.43613043) Å**, with dimensions **26 × 24 × 28 Å**. Vina used `--scoring vina`, exhaustiveness **64**, `--num_modes 20`, `--energy_range 5.0`, `--min_rmsd 1.0`, `--cpu 1`, and seeds **1101, 2202, 3303, 4404, 5505**. All five seeds were run with these fixed settings; every returned pose was saved. Commands, input/output hashes, individual logs, runtime, and pose metrics are in the [results CSV](../../data/metadata/vina_4GQR_glu233_sensitivity_results.csv) and [diagnostic output directory](../../docking/validation/vina/4GQR_Glu233H_sensitivity/).

All **five runs completed**, each returned **20 chemically checked poses**, and no returned Glu233H pose was within **2.0 Å** (best **2.1146 Å**, seed 5505, rank 3). Rank-1 RMSD changed by only **−0.0173 to +0.0131 Å** relative to the corresponding Glu233− E64 seed; the mean changed from **2.5105** to **2.5055 Å**. E64 Glu233− best-of-all-pose RMSDs were **2.1490, 2.1367, 2.1312, 2.1228, and 2.1103 Å** by seed, so the single protonation change did not recover the deposited pose in either top-ranked or best-of-all modes. Vina score differences across microstates are reported for reproducibility, **not** treated as physical affinity differences.

## Decision

**The 4GQR/MYC standard-Vina validation screen remains failed and this 4GQR model should be suspended in its current form.** Glu233H was chemically preparable as a labelled sensitivity receptor, but native-pose recovery was not materially changed across the five prespecified seeds. This does **not** prove Glu233− is the true bound microstate; it only weakens this particular Glu233H explanation for the observed rank-1 failure under the frozen dry/no-NAG setup. Further exhaustiveness increases or serial parameter adjustments to seek a favorable result are not justified by this test.

The important unresolved limitations are the MYC model's **0.40 occupancy**, RSCC **0.662**, RSR **0.56**, and geometry outliers in the [official ligand validation](https://www.rcsb.org/ligand-validation/4gqr/MYC); uncertain phenolic ionization and His201 tautomer; omitted active-site and ion-shell waters; distant linked-NAG omission; and standard Vina's inadequate physical representation of retained Ca/Cl electrostatics and coordination. The proposed Glu233H hydrogen orientation is computational, not experimental. A separately vetted crystallographic active-site reference or an independently validated scoring/preparation method is needed before α-amylase phytochemical pose analysis. Neither a favorable Vina score nor a hypothetical later RMSD improvement would establish biochemical inhibition, binding affinity, or antidiabetic efficacy.
