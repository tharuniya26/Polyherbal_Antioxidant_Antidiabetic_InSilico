# 3L4W phytochemical pilot: docking-box adequacy assessment

**Step 59, 14 October 2026.** This is a controlled search-space sensitivity assessment of the completed mangiferin/quercetin pose-only pilot. The original [Step 58 report](vina_3L4W_phytochemical_pilot_report.md), [40-run results](../../data/metadata/vina_3L4W_phytochemical_pilot_results.csv), raw PDBQT files and classifications remain unchanged. The reproducible [assessment script](../../scripts/assess_vina_3L4W_box_adequacy.py), [original face audit](../../docking/pilot/vina/3L4W_box_adequacy_sensitivity/original_box_face_audit.csv), and timestamp-independent [box preregistration](../../docking/pilot/vina/3L4W_box_adequacy_sensitivity/box_pre_registration.json) specify the comparison before any new search.

## Read-only audit of the original 24 × 18 × 18 Å box

The script independently re-read **all 781** saved Step 58 modes, verified their original file hashes, ligand graphs and mapped coordinates, and calculated distances to all six original box faces directly from the PDBQT coordinates. Every per-pose minimum agreed with the saved Step 58 metric to 0.00001 Å. The original box center was the deposited 14-heavy-atom MIG centroid at **(45.38664286, 92.35157143, 34.82200000) Å**. Its lower Y face was at **83.35157143 Å**. The deposited MIG heavy atoms span Y **89.475–94.054 Å**, leaving at least **6.123 Å** between MIG and that face. Thus proximity of candidate atoms to the Y face is a *search-box* observation; the face is not an experimentally defined edge of the biochemical pocket.

Of the **40 rank-1 poses**, **36** have at least one heavy atom within 1 Å of a box face; the **closest face is Y− for all 40**. Among all **781** modes, **685** are within 1 Å of Y−, **117** within 1 Å of X−, and **25** within 1 Å of Z+ (these face counts overlap); Y− is the closest face for **755** modes and X− for **26**. No saved pose has a heavy atom outside the original box. All ligand heavy atoms, original bonds, and specified mangiferin stereocentres remain present and pass the previous Meeko/RDKit geometry checks. A pose close to a grid face may have restricted search or placement; these coordinate checks alone cannot prove a physical truncation, nor establish where the protein pocket ends.

For mangiferin monoanion/Asp542− the *core itself* is nearest the lower Y face. This justified a single controlled comparison.

## One preregistered larger box

Before any comparison search, the [immutable registration](../../docking/pilot/vina/3L4W_box_adequacy_sensitivity/box_pre_registration.json) fixed the same MIG-based center and a **30 × 24 × 24 Å** box. This adds **3 Å to every original face**. If any original rank-1 coordinates remained unchanged, the least face clearance would increase from **0.115 Å to 3.115 Å**. The added space covers the observed face contacts and the original modes' full-heavy spans, including mangiferin conformations reaching about **13.8 Å** on one axis, without shifting the reference site or selecting dimensions from scores. The resulting box is a sensitivity test, not an optimized production box.

AutoDock Vina 1.2.7 `--score_only` input/grid parsing passed for both receptor states using a hash-verified Step 58 pocket start; those scores were not interpreted. An initial parse check using the archived unpositioned ligand PDBQT failed because its starting coordinates lie outside the centered box. Its error log was preserved; no docking search occurred from that check. The corrected parse check used the exact seeded pocket start that is subsequently copied byte for byte into the comparison run.

The sole planned search change is box size. Both dry/no-NAG receptors, four ligand microstates, ligand graphs and charges, all five seeds, the 40 original randomized start files, `vina` scoring, exhaustiveness 64, requested 20 modes, energy range 5.0, minimum RMSD 1.0 and CPU 1 per run are held fixed. The frozen Step 57 pocket rule, severe-clash thresholds, graph-preserving rigid-core maps, unfitted RMSD medoid definition, and **4/5 rank-1** geometric PASS criterion also remain fixed. See the [pilot plan](vina_3L4W_phytochemical_pilot_plan.md) for those definitions.

## Comparison outcomes

The [40-row comparison results CSV](../../data/metadata/vina_3L4W_box_adequacy_results.csv) links every new run to its original Step 58 seed and records both classifications, both rank-1 margins, status, command, hashes, score, contacts, and unfitted core RMSD. The [all-pose metrics](../../docking/pilot/vina/3L4W_box_adequacy_sensitivity/all_pose_metrics.csv), [comparison face audit](../../docking/pilot/vina/3L4W_box_adequacy_sensitivity/comparison_box_face_audit.csv), and [reproducibility manifest](../../docking/pilot/vina/3L4W_box_adequacy_sensitivity/experiment_manifest.json) preserve all **798** returned modes and their source files.

All **40/40** comparison jobs produced complete, chemically valid multi-mode PDBQT outputs: **38** have confirmed Vina exit code zero and **two** have complete output but an unconfirmed process exit after host suspension (mangiferin neutral/Asp542H, seeds **2202 and 3303**). All **798/798** modes passed Meeko/RDKit graph, heavy-atom identity, mapped-coordinate, stereochemistry where applicable, and bond-geometry checks; no mode placed a ligand heavy atom outside the larger box or met the prespecified severe-clash test. Vina returned 798 rather than 800 modes because its mode filters yielded 19 in two searches. The original Step 58 experiment's *different* two exit-unconfirmed outputs remain unchanged.

An additional parallel tail attempt made **14** quercetin searches fail immediately with Vina's explicit `Error: insufficient memory!`; their original JSON logs are preserved. Those **14** jobs were subsequently repeated only at **two concurrent one-CPU processes**, under separately named `_retry_after_memory` starts, outputs, and logs, with identical seeds, coordinates, chemistry, box and scoring options. All 14 retries completed with confirmed exit-zero status. The CSV retains each failed-attempt log path, hash and error alongside the successful retry. These are technical failures and recoveries, not silently omitted observations. The archived unpositioned-PDBQT `--score_only` parse failure is likewise retained separately from the successful frozen-start grid checks. Neither grid-check score was interpreted.

The larger-box rank-1 near-face count is **1/40**, versus **36/40** originally; across all modes it is **196/798**, versus **685/781**. The new minimum face is Y− for **794/798** modes and Z+ for four. The one near-face rank-1 pose is mangiferin monoanion/Asp542− seed **2202**, at **0.172 Å** from a face; it fails the frozen pocket rule and is **6.500 Å** from that pair's medoid. The other four seeds in that pair localize in the MIG pocket and form a tight rank-1 cluster. Thus the new 4/5 PASS is real under the prospective rule, yet one alternative remains and the state is strongly sensitive to box size.

Mangiferin neutral/Asp542H has **five pocket-localized, clash-free** rank-1 poses in the larger box, but they split into **3 + 2** rigid-core placements. Seeds 1101, 2202 and 3303 form the medoid cluster; seeds 4404 and 5505 are about **2.37 Å** from it and contact His600 within 4 Å, whereas the medoid cluster approaches Asp443 and Asp542 but not His600 within that cutoff. Their Vina scores differ by only hundredths of a scoring unit and do not identify a true bound state. The original 5/5 PASS therefore **does not survive** the box comparison. Mangiferin neutral/Asp542− remains 5/5, but its larger-box cores move about **1.83–1.87 Å** from the corresponding original seed poses; a retained PASS does not imply unchanged geometry. Mangiferin monoanion/Asp542H also remains 5/5 but shifts about **2.43–2.45 Å**. Across receptor states, the mangiferin neutral medoid separation grows **0.146 → 2.381 Å**, while the monoanion separation changes **6.664 → 1.475 Å**. This receptor/box interaction limits any single receptor-state interpretation.

Quercetin neutral rank-1 cores remain in essentially the same off-rule placements after the box is enlarged (same-seed core shifts **0.012–0.087 Å** across the two receptor states). Their clearances improve to roughly **3.6–3.9 Å**, yet none meets the frozen ≥50% MIG-core-overlap rule. A box face therefore does not explain that persistent rank-1 localization. Quercetin monoanion/Asp542− remains 0/5, with multiple rank-1 core placements and no qualifying **rank-1** pocket pose; the Asp542H model remains a 5/5 pocket-localized cluster with **3.48–3.49 Å** rank-1 face clearance and same-seed core shifts only **0.005–0.074 Å** from the original. Its cross-receptor medoid separation stays large (**5.660 → 5.672 Å**), so its pose is stable to the box test *within* the Asp542H hypothesis while remaining receptor-state sensitive. Relevant Asp443, Asp542 and His600 distances for every rank-1 pose and all other modes are in the per-pose table. No Vina score was used to choose a protonation state or infer potency.

## Scientific decision: **BOX-SENSITIVE**

The original search box is **not supported as a geometry-independent setup** for even this limited pose-only pilot. Enlarging it relieved most artificial face proximity but changed **two** of the eight prospective geometric classifications in opposite directions, and changed several mangiferin core placements while keeping their classifications. The unchanged overall **4/8 PASS** count conceals those pair-specific changes. The observations are consistent with search-space sensitivity; they do **not** by themselves isolate whether the cause is steric box restriction, different search coverage at the same exhaustiveness in a larger volume, score ordering, or receptor chemistry. The near-face problem is substantially reduced in the comparison box, but that alone does not validate the comparison setup.

**Next executable task:** keep this 30 × 24 × 24 Å comparison box fixed and perform receptor-specific deposited-MIG native-pose recovery checks in it for the two existing Asp542 receptor microstates, using prespecified seeds and the audited 2 Å crystal-frame RMSD rule. Report any failure without tuning the box or selecting favorable runs. Then decide whether a larger-box phytochemical pilot is qualified for *pose-only* follow-up, explicitly retaining mangiferin's box-dependent clusters and the dry/no-NAG, absent-water, His600 and ligand-ionization uncertainties. A further box size should be chosen only from independent structural reasoning and preregistered as a distinct study, not adjusted to recover a desired PASS count.

**Expansion to all five medicinal plants is not yet justified.** This limited two-chemical test exposes box and receptor-state sensitivity, and none of these docked compounds was analytically demonstrated in the original project's aqueous extracts. Even a future stable pose screen would not establish binding, enzyme inhibition, formulation synergy, measured antidiabetic activity, or comparative potency.
