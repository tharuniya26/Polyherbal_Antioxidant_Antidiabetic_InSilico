# 3L4W phytochemical pose-only pilot: mangiferin and quercetin

**Experiment:** Step 58, 14 October 2026. **Scope:** exploratory geometry and search reproducibility for two literature candidates in human ntMGAM (PDB 3L4W). This is neither an inhibition experiment nor an affinity or potency ranking. The frozen [input plan](vina_3L4W_phytochemical_pilot_plan.md), [input manifest](../../data/metadata/vina_3L4W_pilot_input_manifest.csv), and [validator](../../scripts/validate_vina_3L4W_pilot_inputs.py) define the prospective conditions. The [runner](../../scripts/run_vina_3L4W_phytochemical_pilot.py), [40-run results](../../data/metadata/vina_3L4W_phytochemical_pilot_results.csv), [all-pose table](../../docking/pilot/vina/3L4W_phytochemical_pose_only/all_pose_metrics.csv), and [reproducibility manifest](../../docking/pilot/vina/3L4W_phytochemical_pose_only/experiment_manifest.json) contain the commands, hashes, starts, outputs, and individual measurements.

## Frozen experiment and input gate

The Step 57 validator passed **8/8** ligand-state/receptor-state pairs immediately before docking. Its SHA256 checks covered the ligand PDBQT and source SDF, both receptor PDBQTs and source PDBs, deposited 3L4W/MIG reference, native-control record, Vina executable, and input manifest. The manifest SHA256 was `58c41e3b863cf722677c6429e9af126465333479ee3f3f6c867ef77dd26740fa`. No frozen source input or earlier MIG/4GQR output was modified.

Two dry/no-NAG pH-6.5 receptor models were used: Asp443−/Asp542−/His600-HID and Asp443−/Asp542H(OD2)/His600-HID. For each, neutral and specified monoanionic reference structures of LIG04 (mangiferin) and LIG06 (quercetin) were tested separately. The box was centered on the deposited 14-heavy-atom MIG centroid, `(45.38664286, 92.35157143, 34.82200000) Å`, with dimensions `24 × 18 × 18 Å`. AutoDock Vina 1.2.7 used `vina` scoring, exhaustiveness 64, 20 requested modes, energy range 5.0, minimum RMSD 1.0, and one CPU per run. Each pair used seeds `1101, 2202, 3303, 4404, 5505`.

For each seed and ligand state, a NumPy PCG64 generator keyed by seed, PubChem CID, and charge code generated a random unit-quaternion rigid rotation and a pocket-centered translation with length ≥2 Å. All starting heavy atoms lay at least 0.5 Å inside the frozen box. The same start was used for both receptor states of that ligand state and seed. These are randomized pocket starts for phytochemicals, which have no deposited crystal poses in this complex; the deposited MIG coordinates were used only to define the pocket. Each start, Vina command, captured log, output, runtime, and SHA256 is retained under [`docking/pilot/vina/3L4W_phytochemical_pose_only/`](../../docking/pilot/vina/3L4W_phytochemical_pose_only/).

## Completion and pose quality control

All **40** prespecified searches produced complete multi-model pose files; **38** have a captured Vina exit code of zero. For LIG04 neutral/Asp542− seeds **2202 and 4404**, the host pause caused the runner's wall-clock timeout before a process exit code was captured. Each saved file contains 20 complete returned modes, and its captured output and every pose passed Meeko/RDKit readback; these two are **analyzable outputs with unconfirmed process exit**, not confirmed exit-zero runs. They were retained without rerunning or overwriting. There were **no missing runs or invalid pose files**. Vina returned **781 modes** in total because its score/range and separation filters returned fewer than 20 in some runs.

The runner reconstructed all 781 modes with Meeko/RDKit. It checked the frozen ligand graph, 30 LIG04 or 22 LIG06 heavy-atom identities, mapped coordinates, LIG04 stereocentre geometry, heavy-bond lengths, finite 3D positions, and ascending score rank. All **781/781** passed these chemical and coordinate checks. The [all-pose table](../../docking/pilot/vina/3L4W_phytochemical_pose_only/all_pose_metrics.csv) preserves every score, pocket test, clash count, box margin, catalytic-residue distance, and unfitted rigid-core RMSD. The core maps and graph-allowed equivalent-atom permutations are the frozen Step 57 maps; no pose was fitted to another pose or to MIG before calculating RMSD.

## Prospective geometric screen

Within each pair, the observed rank-1 pose with the smallest summed unfitted rigid-core RMSD to the other four rank-1 poses was the medoid. A seed passed only when its rank-1 pose (1) placed every heavy atom inside the box, its core centroid ≤6 Å from the MIG centroid, and ≥50% of rigid-core atoms within 4 Å of a MIG heavy atom; (2) had no prespecified receptor/ligand or internal severe clash; and (3) was ≤2.0 Å unfitted rigid-core RMSD from that pair's medoid. **PASS required at least four of five seeds.** Scores did not enter the decision.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **LIG04 mangiferin, neutral** | **PASS 5/5** | **PASS 5/5** | All ten rank-1 poses satisfy the MIG-pocket rule and clash screen; maximum within-state core RMSD 0.038 and 0.054 Å, respectively. |
| **LIG04 mangiferin, 6-O monoanion** | **INCONCLUSIVE 0/5** | **PASS 5/5** | Asp542− rank-1 cores are 7.82–7.90 Å from the MIG centroid and only 20% of core atoms meet the 4 Å overlap test. Asp542H rank-1 poses are in the pocket; maximum within-state core RMSD 0.056 Å. |
| **LIG06 quercetin, neutral** | **INCONCLUSIVE 0/5** | **INCONCLUSIVE 0/5** | Rank-1 cores are 5.78–5.95 Å from the MIG centroid, but only 27% of core atoms meet the required 50% overlap. Both states nevertheless converge tightly within their own off-rule pose cluster. |
| **LIG06 quercetin, 4′-O monoanion** | **INCONCLUSIVE 0/5** | **PASS 5/5** | Asp542− rank-1 cores have 27–45% MIG overlap and include one seed at 2.054 Å from the medoid. Asp542H rank-1 poses have 100% overlap; maximum within-state core RMSD 0.065 Å. |

Thus **4/8 combinations PASS**, **4/8 are INCONCLUSIVE**, and **0/8 are technically BLOCKED** under the frozen screen. All 40 rank-1 poses passed the severe-clash test; the all-mode table records six severe-clash modes among 781 returned modes. All rank-1 heavy atoms remained inside the box. **36/40 rank-1 poses and 685/781 modes lie within 1 Å of a box face**, including every rank-1 pose for the three passing LIG04 combinations and the passing LIG06 monoanion/Asp542H combination. The smallest margins in those passing combinations were 0.115–0.479 Å. This substantial edge proximity limits interpretation of the apparently tight pose clusters and warrants an independently specified box-adequacy check before a broader study. The pocket rule was not relaxed after observing these margins.

## Receptor-state comparison and interpretation

The neutral LIG04 medoid core shifts only **0.146 Å** between the two receptor states and all ten rank-1 poses approach Asp443, Asp542, and His600 within 4 Å. For LIG04 monoanion, the cross-state medoid shift is **6.664 Å**: the Asp542− rank-1 cluster lacks contacts within 4 Å of those three residues, whereas the Asp542H cluster contacts all three. For LIG06 monoanion, the cross-state medoid shift is **5.660 Å** and the Asp542H cluster localizes more centrally in the MIG pocket. The neutral LIG06 medoids are **0.145 Å** apart, yet both fail the frozen MIG-core-overlap criterion. These are geometric sensitivities to a protonation hypothesis, not evidence that Asp542H is the actual bound state. The small within-state RMSDs show repeatable search outcomes in this setup; they do not validate the underlying physical scoring model.

The neutral and monoanion hypotheses must remain separate. At pH 6.5, aqueous acid–base evidence makes both LIG04 forms plausible, while the LIG06 4′-O monoanion and its population remain conditional; protein-bound microstates were not measured. Standard Vina scoring does not properly resolve supplied partial-charge electrostatics, so the different raw scores cannot select the correct ligand or receptor protonation state. A lower Vina score for a rank-1 pose outside the frozen pocket criterion is not evidence of a stronger biological effect. Rigid-receptor docking also omits the six linked NAG units, binding-site waters, side-chain motion, alternative His600 microstates, and solvent/polarization effects. The earlier MIG tests are **native-pose placement controls**, not a biochemical validation of these phytochemicals or their affinities. See the [3L4W production-readiness assessment](vina_3L4W_production_readiness.md).

Literature reports [mangiferin in a water extract of *Salacia reticulata* leaves](https://doi.org/10.1271/bbb.80854) and [quercetin from *Senna auriculata* leaves extracted with aqueous acetone and fractionated with ethyl acetate](https://doi.org/10.1248/cpb.c14-00420). **Neither compound was analytically identified in the original project's aqueous leaf extracts.** This pilot establishes neither compound's presence in those extracts nor enzyme binding, inhibition, antidiabetic action, formulation synergy, or comparative potency.

## Decision and next action

The four PASS outcomes support only **qualified, hypothesis-generating pocket-pose exploration** under their specific frozen input models. The four INCONCLUSIVE outcomes must be retained as such; in particular, receptor-state dependent pocket localization prevents a single robust conclusion for either anion. Before expanding to more phytochemicals or interpreting pose contacts, perform one **predeclared box-adequacy diagnostic** on the existing pilot states, because 36/40 rank-1 poses approach a box face, and independently assess relevant protonation/solvent assumptions. Preserve this original experiment as the fixed baseline, use the same pocket and clash tests, and report any changed geometry as sensitivity rather than retroactively changing these PASS/INCONCLUSIVE calls. Do not use these scores for potency ranking or infer that the original aqueous extracts contained these chemicals.
