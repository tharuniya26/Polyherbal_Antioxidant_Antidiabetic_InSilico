The frozen severe-clash rule and symmetry-aware **unfitted**, receptor-frame core-RMSD-to-medoid threshold of **2.0 Å** were applied without relaxation. A receptor condition needs **at least four of five rank-1 poses** to pass all three tests. All ten rank-1 poses were outside the MIG-pocket rule; none had a severe *modelled receptor* or internal heavy-atom clash. Therefore both conditions are **INCONCLUSIVE**, independent of any core clustering.

### Alternative modes and structural diagnostics

Of the **200 valid returned modes**, **13** satisfy pocket membership: **6/100** with Asp542− and **7/100** with Asp542H(OD2). None is rank 1. A pocket-localized alternative appeared in three Asp542− runs (first at ranks **18, 12, 11** for seeds 2202, 3303, 4404) and four Asp542H runs (first at ranks **17, 6, 5, 5** for seeds 1101, 2202, 3303, 4404). Seeds **1101 and 5505** in Asp542− and **5505** in Asp542H returned no pocket-member among 20 modes. The first pocket-member scored **0.292–0.623 Vina units** worse than its run's rank-1 mode. These secondary poses show the search can find MIG-localized geometries in some runs; they do not satisfy the frozen rank-1 criterion or establish binding.

No returned mode tripped the frozen modelled-receptor/internal severe-clash screen. The nearest search-box face was **Y− for every rank-1 pose**. One rank-1 pose, Asp542H seed 2202, was **<1.0 Å** from that face; **15/200** modes had a sub-1-Å face margin overall (7 Asp542−, 8 Asp542H). The original search box is therefore not treated as an observed biochemical boundary, and the face warning is retained even though the enlarged box passed native-MIG redocking.

The read-only comparison with **434 oxygen records in the original deposited 3L4W PDB** found a ligand-heavy-atom approach **<1.8 Å** to a deposited water oxygen in **all ten rank-1 poses** and **186/200** modes (95 Asp542−, 91 Asp542H). The smallest rank-1 approach was **0.6289 Å**; individual water IDs and distances are in the run and all-pose tables. These are serious overlaps with omitted crystallographic waters in the dry receptor hypothesis. They are **not** contacts to waters present in the Vina receptor and do **not** retroactively change the frozen PASS rule. Static crystal waters also do not establish how water would reorient or displace in solution. The dry-model results require caution; a water-aware calculation would need a separately justified method and fresh validation.

## Scientific interpretation and next action

The completed pilot establishes **technical input and pose-output integrity** but **does not support reproducible top-ranked MIG-pocket placement of this neutral PubChem agnuside reference** in either tested dry/no-NAG 3L4W microstate. The two receptor variants differ in some pose locations and secondary-mode ranks, yet both independently fail the same 4/5 screen. The lower-ranked pocket modes and rank-1 water overlaps warrant structural examination; changing the box, selecting favorable ranks, removing more components or comparing raw scores would not repair this prospective result.

The molecule is a **PubChem stereodefined reference**, not an analyte whose exact nine stereocentres were confirmed by the cited *Vitex negundo* leaf work. Full direct water-only leaf chromatographic evidence remains unavailable, and agnuside was **not analytically established in this project's original aqueous extracts**. Both receptors omit linked NAG glycans and crystallographic waters, and Asp542/His600 protonation remains provisional. Native-MIG pose recovery in this box tests a different, smaller ligand and does not establish glycoside sampling, physical scoring accuracy, affinity, enzyme inhibition, antidiabetic efficacy or formulation synergy. **LIG03 trans-caffeic acid remains on its prior botanical/isomer HOLD and was not docked.** No ADMET or molecular dynamics was run.

**Next executable task:** perform a *read-only* structural audit of the recurrent agnuside rank-1 placements and the 13 secondary MIG-pocket modes against deposited waters, receptor surface residues and omitted NAG positions. Then decide, before any new search, whether a single independent and chemically justified water/conformer-sensitivity experiment is feasible. Retain this pilot's INCONCLUSIVE classification and all original outputs regardless of that decision.
