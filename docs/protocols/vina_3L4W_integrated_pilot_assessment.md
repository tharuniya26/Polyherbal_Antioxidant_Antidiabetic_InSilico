## Audit and prospective rule

The same two dry/no-NAG 3L4W receptor hypotheses were used throughout: Asp443−/Asp542−/His600-HID and Asp443−/Asp542H(OD2)/His600-HID, with one neutral and one separately specified monoanionic hypothesis for each of LIG04 mangiferin and LIG06 quercetin. The original box was **24 × 18 × 18 Å**; the sole prespecified comparison was **30 × 24 × 24 Å**, with the unchanged deposited-MIG centroid **(45.38664286, 92.35157143, 34.82200000) Å**. Both experiments used AutoDock Vina 1.2.7, exhaustiveness 64, 20 requested modes, energy range 5.0, minimum RMSD 1.0, and seeds 1101, 2202, 3303, 4404, 5505 for each combination. The enlarged-box [neutral native-MIG control](../../data/metadata/vina_3L4W_large_box_mig_results.csv) completed **10/10** confirmed runs: both receptors recovered a rank-1 pose within **2.0 Å** of the deposited MIG reference in **5/5 seeds**. This qualifies a limited native-pose placement check in the larger search space; it does not establish correct receptor or ligand chemistry.

The original *candidate* rule was applied unchanged. A rank-1 pose must have every ligand heavy atom inside the box; rigid-core centroid ≤6.0 Å from the deposited MIG centroid; ≥50% of frozen rigid-core atoms within 4.0 Å of a deposited MIG heavy atom; no receptor–ligand heavy pair <1.8 Å or van-der-Waals overlap >0.8 Å; no specified nonbonded internal heavy pair <1.6 Å; and ≤2.0 Å **unfitted** rigid-core RMSD from the observed within-combination rank-1 medoid. A combination passes only if **at least 4/5** prespecified rank-1 seeds pass all tests. The core has 15 heavy atoms for mangiferin and 11 for quercetin. These are geometric, not affinity, criteria. A catalytic residue listed within 4 Å is a distance contact, not an assigned hydrogen bond or inhibition mechanism.

## Structural interpretation of the enlarged-box poses

**Mangiferin neutral.** On Asp542−, all five enlarged rank-1 poses remain in the frozen pocket, with 100% core overlap, no severe clash, and Asp443/Asp542/His600 heavy-atom proximity within 4 Å. The original same-seed cores move roughly 1.8 Å when the box grows; consistent *site localization* therefore does not imply the same detailed atomic pose. On Asp542H(OD2), seeds **1101, 2202 and 3303** form the medoid cluster. Seeds **4404 and 5505** are **2.3663 and 2.3680 Å** from that medoid, respectively. All five have 100% core overlap and no severe clash. The three-seed group approaches Asp443/Asp542 but has no His600 heavy atom within 4 Å; the two-seed group also approaches His600. Their rank-1 Vina scores differ by only hundredths of a scoring unit and provide no defensible selection of the biological pose. The neutral cross-receptor medoid difference rises from **0.146 Å** in the original box to **2.381 Å** in the enlarged box. The enlarged Asp542H condition is therefore **INCONCLUSIVE**, even though its five poses occupy the broad MIG site.

**Mangiferin monoanion.** On Asp542−, enlarged-box seeds **1101, 3303, 4404 and 5505** meet the frozen pocket, clash and core-cluster tests. Seed **2202** does not: its core centroid is **7.0097 Å** from the MIG centroid, only **26.67%** of its core atoms overlap the MIG neighborhood, and its core is **6.4997 Å** from the medoid. Its nearest box-face clearance is **0.1724 Å**. It has Asp443/Asp542/His600 *distance* contacts because other atoms extend toward the site; that does not override the frozen rigid-core pocket rule. The 4/5 PASS is legitimate under the prospective rule but is strongly box sensitive relative to 0/5 originally. On Asp542H(OD2), all five seeds pass in both boxes, yet the core shift and disappearance of a <4 Å His600 contact mean a single detailed contact geometry is not robust. The monoanion cross-receptor medoid separation changes **6.664 → 1.475 Å** with box size. Neither pattern identifies the actual ionization state at pH 6.5 or in the enzyme.

**Quercetin neutral.** All ten enlarged rank-1 poses form tight within-receptor clusters and are clash free. Their rigid-core overlap with the deposited MIG neighborhood is only **27.27%**, below the frozen 50% threshold, on both receptors. Larger-box rank-1 face clearance improves to **3.638–3.860 Å**. Thus proximity to the original box edge does not explain the persistent off-rule classification. The rank-1 poses can be reported as sampled placements, but they should **not** be interpreted as reproducible poses in the prespecified MIG competitive pocket. Their catalytic-residue distance contacts do not change that conclusion, nor does the off-rule result show that quercetin is biologically inactive.

**Quercetin 4′-O monoanion.** On Asp542−, no enlarged rank-1 pose meets the pocket test: core overlap is **27.27–45.45%**, and two rank-1 cores exceed 2 Å from the medoid. On Asp542H(OD2), all five rank-1 poses have **100%** core overlap, no severe clash, **3.480–3.488 Å** nearest-face clearance and same-seed core shifts of only **0.005–0.074 Å** across boxes. This is a repeatable exploratory site placement *within one receptor and one hypothesized anion*. Its receptor-state medoids remain **5.660 Å** apart in the original box and **5.672 Å** apart in the enlarged box. The pose cannot be generalized to Asp542− or to unmeasured neutral/anion populations; raw Vina scores cannot select those microstates.

No chemical microstate is **globally stable across both receptor hypotheses and both boxes** under the original geometric rule. Two *particular* submodels—mangiferin neutral/Asp542− and quercetin monoanion/Asp542H—retain 5/5 calls in both boxes; only the latter also retains nearly unchanged same-seed core positions. A persistent PASS is still a within-model geometric observation, not proof of binding or of the correct phenolic/catalytic protonation.

**No further docking diagnostic is required to report these existing observations with their sensitivity limits.** Repeating search settings or choosing a third box from scores would not resolve the unknown aqueous-extract chemistry or receptor/ligand protonation. A future diagnostic must be motivated by independent chemical or structural evidence and preregistered separately. The enlarged box is **qualified as one tested search space for limited pose-only work**, because native neutral MIG recovered on both receptor models and candidate box-edge crowding was markedly reduced. It is not established as an optimal, box-independent production setup. The dry receptors omit six linked NAG units and active-site waters; His600-HID and Asp542 states are hypotheses, the 6-O and 4′-O phenolates are hypothesized solution/bound microstates, and ordinary Vina cannot validate proton-coupled electrostatics, water exchange or binding free energies.

Broad expansion to the remaining phytochemicals from all five plants is **not yet justified**. The [preliminary library](../../data/compounds/final_preliminary_ligand_library.csv) has **10 assigned structures across 12 plant–compound associations**, including conditional chemical and botanical records, plus an identity-unresolved Aegle assignment. Only LIG04/LIG06 and their specified states passed the present ligand-specific input gate. Published occurrence evidence must remain separate from any original extract detection: **neither mangiferin nor quercetin was analytically confirmed in this project's original aqueous extracts**. The former has published water-leaf occurrence in *Salacia reticulata*; the latter's leaf isolation from *Senna auriculata* used **80% aqueous acetone**, not water-only extraction. No reported pose establishes inhibition, relative potency, antioxidant activity, antidiabetic efficacy or formulation synergy.

### Minimum gates before one further candidate

1. Select **one** additional plant-associated structure on documented compound-specific botanical evidence. Keep any source plant-part, extraction-solvent, stereochemical or exact-identity uncertainty visible; do not promote an identity-hold association or assume original-extract detection.
2. Audit its CID-backed molecular graph, stereochemistry, conformer pool, source SDF and Meeko PDBQT readback; document plausible pH-6.5 ionization/tautomer hypotheses, charges, torsions and every file hash. Put an unresolved identity or unsupported conversion on **HOLD** rather than substituting a convenient isomer.
3. Prespecify the same two hash-frozen dry/no-NAG receptor states, **30 × 24 × 24 Å** MIG-centered box, Vina settings, five seeds and ligand-specific rigid-core map before any new search. Check the new ligand's size and randomized starts for box fit and Vina input compatibility; retain the native-MIG control by hash without repeating it absent an input change.
4. Apply the original pocket, severe-clash, unfitted medoid-core and ≥4/5 rank-1 rule in **each** receptor state. Preserve every mode, failure, exit uncertainty and <1 Å face warning; report state/box limitations and never select a chemical state by score alone. A new near-face or split-cluster result remains conditional and must not be made favorable by post hoc box tuning.
