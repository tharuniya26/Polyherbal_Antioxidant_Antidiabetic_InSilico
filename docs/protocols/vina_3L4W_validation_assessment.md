# 3L4W/MIG search robustness and chemical sensitivity assessment

**Scope:** controlled follow-up to the five-seed [exhaustiveness-32 diagnostic redocking](vina_3L4W_redocking_report.md) of native miglitol (MIG) into human N-terminal maltase-glucoamylase [3L4W](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/3L4W). The native crystal SDF, original structures, previous outputs and atom maps were preserved. This assessment tests pose recovery and sensitivity of a **dry, no-NAG local-pocket receptor model**; it does not establish inhibition potency or validate the complete glycosylated biological structure.

## Frozen inputs and case selection

The prespecified neutral baseline uses the previously audited Asp443⁻/Asp542⁻/His600-HID receptor at preparation pH 6.5 and the neutral [MIG CCD-reference PDBQT](../../docking/validation/vina/3L4W_MIG_crystal_pose_reference.pdbqt). The [original crystal SDF](../../docking/validation/vina/3L4W_MIG_native_pose.sdf) remains the sole RMSD reference. A second eligible case uses the **same receptor** with the previously prepared MIG amine +1 PDBQT as an explicit microstate sensitivity hypothesis. The [chemistry review](vina_3L4W_chemistry_review.md) documents PROPKA predictions and the limitations of these choices. Both cases share the exact deposited-ligand-derived box: center **(45.38664286, 92.35157143, 34.82200000) Å**, dimensions **24 × 18 × 18 Å**.

Before either case, the [reproducibility script](../../scripts/run_vina_3L4W_sensitivity.py) checks source hashes against the chemistry review, checks all five previous outputs and logs against their recorded hashes, verifies 14 MIG heavy atoms and unchanged deposited heavy coordinates, and reads ligand graph, charge and carbon stereochemistry with RDKit and Meeko. It derives the charged PDBQT-to-SDF atom map from unique deposited heavy coordinates; the neutral map must equal the previously audited map. The charged SDF has a defined modeled configuration at the protonated nitrogen. Meeko's reconstructed SMILES omits that nitrogen stereochemical tag, although its coordinates preserve the SDF tetrahedral geometry. The script therefore checks the signed N–three-carbon–H tetrahedron against the charged SDF in the input, randomized start and **every returned charged pose**. This tests the chosen geometrical model; the nitrogen configuration and bound amine charge were not determined experimentally.

The two-water HID receptor was hash-checked but **excluded from standard rigid-Vina scoring**. HOH A4002/A4004 are deposited near MIG, yet their prepared O–H geometry and orientation, occupancy during binding and exchange are not established; fixed waters are not a defensible validated solvent model here ([Vina's distinct hydrated-docking workflow](https://autodock-vina.readthedocs.io/en/stable/docking_hydrated.html)). The earlier automatically doubly protonated His600 receptor was not adopted as an alternative: that state arose from an automated default without crystallographic support, while a trial HIE placement showed a local hydrogen clash. A protonated Asp542 receptor and a fully glycan-retaining Meeko receptor do not exist as validated inputs. These are unresolved chemistry questions, not negative docking results. The six omitted NAG units are covalently linked, and the no-NAG results apply only to this local sensitivity model.

## Search and pose-validation method

Each case uses seeds **1101, 2202, 3303, 4404 and 5505** with Vina 1.2.7, `--scoring vina`, `--exhaustiveness 64`, `--num_modes 20`, `--energy_range 5.0`, `--min_rmsd 1.0` and `--cpu 1`. The randomized starting PDBQT is generated with NumPy PCG64 from each seed by a rigid random rotation and translation, at least 5 Å unfitted heavy-atom RMSD from the deposited pose. No crystal pose is scored or minimized as a substitute for redocking. Search settings, command lines, software versions and SHA256 hashes are recorded in the [manifest](../../docking/validation/vina/3L4W_sensitivity/experiment_manifest.json), [results CSV](../../data/metadata/vina_3L4W_sensitivity_results.csv) and individual logs. The `--charged-only` script option runs only the charged case to permit independent execution; running the script without it verifies or reanalyses existing artifacts and assembles the complete results table.

Every returned PDBQT mode is parsed with Meeko, its molecular graph and 3D geometry are checked, and its 14 heavy atoms are compared to the original MIG crystal SDF through the audited map. RMSD is symmetry-aware and calculated in the common receptor coordinate frame **without fitting each ligand pose to the crystal**. The neutral and charged ligands share the same deposited heavy-atom reference. The conventional 2.0 Å rank-1 cutoff is a pose-recovery screen, not a free-energy or biochemical validation threshold. Rank-1 RMSD and best RMSD across all returned poses are reported separately for every seed.

The neutral-ligand search repeated all five prespecified seeds successfully. The table separates the top-ranked pose from the pose with the lowest crystal-frame RMSD among *all* returned poses. Vina scores are dimensioned as kcal/mol in its output but are not measured binding free energies.

For neutral MIG, **rank-1 recovery was 5/5 at both E32 and E64** using the initial 2.0 Å screen. The closer approximately 0.7 Å alternative occurred at rank 2 in **3/5 E32 runs** and **4/5 E64 runs**; seed 3303 did not return that alternative. E64 returned 98 modes in total, of which 9 were below 2 Å. The rank-1 RMSD interval stayed 1.8629–1.8849 Å at both settings, and rank-1 scores changed at most 0.007 kcal/mol for any seed. For E64, the more crystal-like rank-2 pose scored 0.104–0.182 kcal/mol worse than rank 1 in the four seeds where it appeared. Thus the **favored mode is search-converged** under these two tested exhaustiveness settings, while discovery/ranking of the more crystal-like secondary mode remains seed dependent. E128 was not invoked: the higher setting did not materially change rank-1 recovery, and the remaining limitation is primarily the scoring preference and model chemistry, not lack of a reproducible rank-1 search result.

For this **one modeled protonated-MIG geometry**, rank-1 and any-pose recovery were both **5/5** at ≤2 Å. The approximately 0.61–0.62 Å pose appeared consistently at rank 2, while the rank-1 pose ranged from 1.8302 to 1.8759 Å. Vina favored rank 1 over the more crystal-like rank 2 by 0.043–0.102 kcal/mol. The charged case returned 99 modes, 10 below 2 Å. Every returned charged pose passed the graph, carbon-stereochemistry, heavy-atom map and explicit nitrogen-geometry checks. The smallest cross-seed rank-1 pairwise heavy-atom RMSD was 0.0319 Å and the largest was 0.0938 Å; for neutral MIG the corresponding range was 0.0063–0.0358 Å. These are same-frame, mapped comparisons without pose fitting.

Across both cases, **10/10 Vina runs completed, zero docking runs failed, and 197 poses were returned**. The single first-pass analysis interruption occurred before any charged docking: a direct SDF-versus-Meeko SMILES comparison revealed that the charged SDF carried an R tag on nitrogen while Meeko's PDBQT readback omitted that tag. The script was amended to require matching charged N tetrahedral geometry in the input, randomized start and every docked pose, while still requiring identical carbon stereochemistry and molecular graph. The five completed neutral Vina runs were preserved and reanalysed; none was rerun or selected away. The untagged PDBQT metadata and the lack of experimental bound-N assignment remain limitations, even though the geometrical check passed for this particular prepared hypothesis.

The complete [10-row results table](../../data/metadata/vina_3L4W_sensitivity_results.csv) and [197-row pose table](../../docking/validation/vina/3L4W_sensitivity/all_pose_metrics.csv) preserve successes and alternative poses. The requested `num_modes 20` was not guaranteed by Vina: neutral seeds 2202/4404 and charged seed 5505 each returned 19. All output, log and randomized-start hashes were independently rechecked against the results CSV. The [original E32 data](../../data/metadata/vina_3L4W_redocking_results.csv) were not changed.

## Interpretation and decision

**Search convergence is acceptable for the tested dry receptor:** the rank-1 neutral mode remained below 2 Å and geometrically stable across all seeds at both E32 and E64; the charged hypothesis also recovered a tightly clustered rank-1 mode across five seeds. No E128 run was justified by the rank-1 convergence results. The appearance of a more crystal-like rank-2 pose in four neutral and all five charged E64 runs shows a persistent **score-ordering limitation**. More search alone cannot establish that the rank-1 pose or either protonation state is physically correct.

**Native-pose recovery is robust only across the two tested ligand inputs, not across receptor microstates.** The receptor remained Asp443⁻/Asp542⁻/His600-HID, dry and no-NAG in every new run. Asp542 protonation, histidine tautomer, explicit structural waters and complete N-linked glycans have not passed preparation/scoring gates. The protonated ligand test fixes one model-specific nitrogen geometry; the crystal does not assign that protonation or configuration. Vina's [standard scoring does not use the supplied partial charges as a physical electrostatic model](https://autodock-vina.readthedocs.io/en/stable/faq.html), so the neutral and charged score difference cannot determine a bound-state pKa or affinity preference.

**Recommendation:** 3L4W can serve as a *qualified, hypothesis-generating ntMGAM pocket model* for a later exploratory pose-placement study, explicitly labelled **dry/no-NAG, provisional pH-6.5 catalytic microstate**. Its present validation supports recovering the deposited MIG heavy-atom pose in this local model; it does **not** justify comparative phytochemical potency ranking, production scoring, cross-receptor transfer, or biochemical claims. Before interpreting a phytochemical series, prepare and test chemically sound Asp542/histidine and glycan-retaining variants where feasible, examine water sensitivity with a defensible method, and use receptor-specific experimental controls. Report failures and state dependence. No production phytochemical docking or ADMET was performed here.
