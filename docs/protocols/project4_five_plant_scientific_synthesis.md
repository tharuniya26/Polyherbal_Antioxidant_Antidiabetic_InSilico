## Source boundary and audit

The original dissertation itself, its bibliography, raw assay measurements and complete extraction protocol are **not deposited** in the present repository. The available experimental record is the [README](../../README.md) and [formulation transcription](../../config/formulations.yaml). They describe aqueous leaf extracts of all five plants, mixed into C1, C2 and C3, and phytochemical screening, TPC, TFC, TAC, DPPH, ABTS, glucose adsorption and yeast glucose uptake. The README qualitatively reports C1 strongest for DPPH, C2 generally highest for glucose adsorption, C3 strongest for ABTS and generally high for yeast uptake. No original numerical value, replicate, uncertainty, statistical test or compound-specific concentration can be audited here. These statements are reported background, not independently recalculated findings.

The library has **12 botanical association rows: 11 assigned associations representing 10 unique reference structures, plus one identity-unresolved Aegle kaempferol-rutinoside row**. Rutin is LIG02 for both Aegle and Senna and should be modelled once. An earlier Step 61 sentence describes “10 assigned structures across 12 associations”; that includes the unresolved row in its association count. The precise distinction here follows the current CSV. C1 and C2 include all five plants; C3 includes Senna, Salacia and Vitex. Ratios are aqueous *extract* proportions, not concentrations of gallic acid, mangiferin or any other named molecule. **No individual candidate was analytically identified in the project's original aqueous extracts.**

Completed result tables were checked for row counts, statuses and predefined outcomes. The original 24 × 18 × 18 Å mangiferin/quercetin pilot has **40 usable outputs, 781 modes**, including **38 confirmed zero exits and two complete outputs with unconfirmed process exits**. Its single 30 × 24 × 24 Å comparison has **40 usable outputs, 798 modes**, again **38 confirmed and two different exit-unconfirmed outputs**; 14 separate memory-failed attempts had same-input retries. Gallic acid has **20/20 confirmed final runs, 382 modes**, with two earlier interrupted attempts retained separately. Agnuside has **10/10 confirmed final runs, 200 modes**, with two interrupted seed-4404 attempts retained separately. These technical histories are not additional successful scientific runs. The [integrated pilot](vina_3L4W_integrated_pilot_assessment.md), [box audit](vina_3L4W_box_adequacy_report.md), [gallic review](vina_3L4W_gallic_acid_off_pocket_investigation.md), and [agnuside review](vina_3L4W_agnuside_structural_investigation.md) contain pose-level hash and geometry audits. No numerical discrepancy requiring source-result correction was found in the current run tables.

## Target controls and their limits

Human ntMGAM **3L4W/MIG** is the tested α-glucosidase-related model. The two pH-6.5 **dry/no-NAG** receptor hypotheses are Asp443−/Asp542−/His600-HID and Asp443−/Asp542H(OD2)/His600-HID. The original-box neutral MIG control recovered the deposited pose at rank 1 within **2.0 Å in 5/5 seeds per receptor**. The enlarged **30 × 24 × 24 Å** box, centred at the deposited MIG centroid `(45.38664286, 92.35157143, 34.82200000) Å`, also achieved **10/10** confirmed rank-1 recoveries, with RMSDs **1.8671–1.9362 Å**. Some more crystal-like secondary poses ranked below rank 1. Ligand-ammonium and Asp542-protonation sensitivity tests likewise met the limited pose screen in the original box. This is positive **native-pose recovery**, not experimental inhibition, physical score validation or proof that omitting glycans/waters is harmless. See the [target summary](vina_target_validation_summary.md) and [large-box control](vina_3L4W_large_box_mig_validation.md).

The human pancreatic α-amylase **4GQR/MYC** model failed the same prospective rank-1 ≤2 Å criterion: **0/5 at E32, 0/5 at E64, and 0/5 after the single Glu233H sensitivity change**. It remains **suspended for phytochemical docking**. MYC's deposited occupancy, local reference uncertainty, waters, Ca/Cl scoring and protonation complicate interpretation, but no demonstrated cause rescues its failed validation. An E32 rank-17 native-like alternative is not a rank-1 success. The [failure investigation](vina_4GQR_failure_investigation.md) and [Glu233H report](vina_4GQR_glu233_sensitivity_report.md) preserve the negative result. Neither 1XD0 nor 2QMJ was advanced to a validated production α-amylase comparison.

## Phytochemical pose observations

The prospectively frozen candidate rule required all heavy atoms inside the search box, the mapped rigid-core centroid ≤6 Å from the deposited MIG centroid, ≥50% core-atom overlap within 4 Å of deposited MIG heavy atoms, no severe clash, and unfitted symmetry-aware core RMSD ≤2 Å to the within-condition rank-1 medoid for **at least 4/5 prespecified seeds**. **PASS means repeatable geometry in that computational condition only.** Lower-ranked pocket modes do not change a rank-1 failure.

| Ligand state | Asp542− original → enlarged | Asp542H(OD2) original → enlarged | Scientific reading |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mangiferin neutral | PASS 5/5 → PASS 5/5 | PASS 5/5 → INCONCLUSIVE 3/5 | Site localization persists for Asp542−, but atomic placement moves; Asp542H develops a 3+2 core split. |
| Mangiferin 6-O monoanion | INCONCLUSIVE 0/5 → PASS 4/5 | PASS 5/5 → PASS 5/5 | Strong box dependence for Asp542−, including one off-pocket seed; pose geometry changes in Asp542H. |
| Quercetin neutral | INCONCLUSIVE 0/5 → INCONCLUSIVE 0/5 | INCONCLUSIVE 0/5 → INCONCLUSIVE 0/5 | Tight but persistently outside frozen MIG-core-overlap rule. |
| Quercetin 4′-O monoanion | INCONCLUSIVE 0/5 → INCONCLUSIVE 0/5 | PASS 5/5 → PASS 5/5 | Reproducible pocket placement only for the Asp542H receptor hypothesis. |

Thus **4/8 combinations pass in each box, but two individual calls change**. Original-box face proximity affected **36/40** rank-1 poses; the enlarged comparison reduced this to **1/40** without making every candidate result stable. The enlarged box is supported for **limited pose-only work** by the MIG control; it is **box sensitive**, not an optimized or general-purpose binding-affinity protocol. Do not select a receptor, charge state or pose solely by the most favorable Vina score.

**Gallic acid:** neutral acid and carboxylate each yielded **INCONCLUSIVE 0/5** on both receptors in the enlarged box. All 20 rank-1 poses crowd the **Z− face**, 0.008–0.067 Å away, and are outside the frozen MIG pocket. Every run also sampled a lower-ranked pocket pose (first at rank 2 or 3); the within-run Vina score gap is only 0.019–0.069 units. The off-pocket rank-1 site conflicts geometrically with six deposited water oxygens that were omitted from the dry receptor; the first pocket alternatives do not show that overlap. This is an observed ranking and omitted-solvent issue, not evidence of inactivity or a validated alternate binding site.

**Agnuside:** the neutral 33-heavy-atom PubChem reference yielded **INCONCLUSIVE 0/5** on both receptors. Its ten rank-1 cores lie outside the frozen MIG-pocket rule, often near Asp327/Asp203/Gln603. Thirteen of 200 lower-ranked modes meet the pocket rule: **6** with Asp542− and **7** with Asp542H. All ten rank-1 poses and **12/13** pocket alternatives approach a deposited water oxygen within 1.8 Å, although those waters are absent from the dry scored receptor. The closest omitted linked NAG atom is >7 Å from each of these 23 poses. Five distinct conformers were assigned to five different seeds, so conformer choice and search variability are confounded. Neither apparent catalytic proximity nor the lower-ranked modes establishes binding.

## Defensible synthesis and unresolved work

The project can report: the original study's **qualitative formulation-level** in-vitro comparisons as unverified source summaries; a transparent, unequal-strength **five-plant literature candidate library**; successful **3L4W/MIG pose-recovery controls** for two tested dry receptor hypotheses; **failed 4GQR/MYC validation**; and the exact frozen, conditional, inconclusive or box-sensitive **pose-placement** outcomes above. It cannot claim that a specific candidate was present in the original extract, explain DPPH/ABTS or glucose responses through these poses, rank plant compounds by inhibition, establish antidiabetic efficacy, infer formulation synergy, or equate Vina scores with measured affinities. No ADMET, antioxidant quantum-chemical study or molecular dynamics result is available.

Remaining scientific limitations are the missing original dissertation/raw data; solvent and part mismatches for several candidates; unresolved E/Z, glycoside or optical-isomer assignments; unknown extract concentrations and bound microstates; dry/no-NAG rigid receptor models, omitted active-site waters and provisional Asp542/His600 states; small glycoside conformer samples; and the failed independent α-amylase control. These limitations are **not** repaired by the native MIG screen. No further agnuside or gallic-acid docking is justified by the current read-only investigations alone.
