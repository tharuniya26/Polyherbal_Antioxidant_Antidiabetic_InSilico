# Receptor coordinate preparation and native inhibitor recovery

**Status (11 October 2026):** Native inhibitor chemistry and deposited heavy-atom poses are validated. The ligand-free receptor coordinate copies are **intermediate working files**, not protonated or GOLD-validated receptors. No docking or scoring has been performed.

## Sources and reproducibility

The original [2QMJ](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/2QMJ) and [1XD0](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/1XD0) PDB files remain unchanged in [`structures/proteins/original/`](../../structures/proteins/original/). The corresponding official [2QMJ mmCIF](https://files.rcsb.org/download/2QMJ.cif) and [1XD0 mmCIF](https://files.rcsb.org/download/1XD0.cif) files were also retained unchanged. The [RCSB download service](https://www.rcsb.org/docs/programmatic-access/file-download-services) supplied coordinate and definition files. The machine-readable [preparation results](../../data/metadata/receptor_preparation_results.csv) record their URLs, SHA-256 checksums, derived-file checksums, and validation outcomes. The reproducible reconstruction program is [`scripts/prepare_native_redocking.py`](../../scripts/prepare_native_redocking.py); it uses RDKit and Gemmi and stops on graph, identity, atom-name, stereochemical, or coordinate mismatch.

## 2QMJ: complete alpha-acarbose

The deposited inhibitor is the **single connected** BIRD entity [PRD_900007, alpha-acarbose](https://www1.rcsb.org/ligand/PRD_900007), not three independently dockable sugars. Its PDB representation is GLC B1–GLC B2–AC1 B3. The official [PRD definition](https://files.rcsb.org/birds/download/PRD_900007.cif), [whole-entity chemical definition](https://files.rcsb.org/birds/download/PRDCC_900007.cif), [GLC definition](https://files.rcsb.org/ligands/download/GLC.cif), and [AC1 definition](https://files.rcsb.org/ligands/download/AC1.cif) were compared with deposited component atoms and PDB `LINK` records. The links are GLC B1 O4–GLC B2 C1 and GLC B2 O4–AC1 B3 C1. The linked GLC B2 and AC1 lack component O1 leaving-group atoms as expected; those atoms must not be invented or added. All **44 expected heavy atoms** are present. The reconstructed single graph has formula **C25H43NO18** and InChIKey `XUFXOAAUWZOOIT-WVJZLWNXSA-N`, matching the official whole-entity definition. All **19 assigned stereocentres** agree with the official stereodefined graph; none is unassigned in the reference SDF.

The output [`2QMJ_PRD_900007_native_pose.sdf`](../../docking/validation/2QMJ_PRD_900007_native_pose.sdf) is one connected molecule with 3D coordinates. Its deposited **heavy-atom coordinates** agree with the original PDB and mmCIF within 0.0011 Å, allowing for file rounding. Explicit hydrogens were generated computationally and are **not experimental coordinates**. The corresponding [atom map](../../docking/validation/2QMJ_PRD_900007_atom_map.csv) links each 1-based SDF heavy-atom index to its deposited residue and atom name. The arithmetic heavy-atom centroid is **(−20.808, −6.586, −5.074) Å**; this is a starting site locator, not an approved GOLD sphere. The working [ligand-free receptor coordinate copy](../../structures/proteins/prepared/2QMJ_working_no_native_ligand.pdb) removes only the 44 PRD atoms.

Protein chain A has 863 modeled of 870 construct residues; residues 1–6 and 837 are absent, away from the recorded catalytic site. Alternate positions occur at Arg68, Cys479, Glu661, Asn741, Lys762, and NAG A2001. The closest protein alternate to the inhibitor is about 10.2 Å away. All **626 deposited waters**, NAG units, nine GOL molecules, and sulfate remain in the working copy pending review. NAG A2005 is about 3.9 Å from inhibitor and needs an explicit retain/remove decision. The catalytic site includes Asp443 and Asp542; Asp203, Thr205, Asp327, Arg526, and His600 help locate its inhibitor-contact region ([structure study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jmb.2007.10.069)). Review distal-sugar density and B factors before interpreting full-ligand pose recovery. The initial structural audit found no inhibitor–protein crystal-mate contact within 4 Å.

## 1XD0: deposited ARE product

The native ligand is [ARE, an acarbose-derived pentasaccharide](https://www.rcsb.org/ligand/ARE), **not intact acarbose**. Its full deposited ARE A501 atom set and bonds were matched to the official [ARE chemical component](https://files.rcsb.org/ligands/download/ARE.cif). All **55 expected heavy atoms** are present as one connected graph. Formula **C31H53NO23**, InChIKey `JUCZMZWYUIRMPL-NPPSNIQKSA-N`, and all **24 assigned stereocentres** match the official definition; none is unassigned in the reference SDF. ARE's deposited heavy-atom coordinates agree with the original PDB and mmCIF within 0.0011 Å. Generated hydrogen coordinates are computational.

The output [`1XD0_ARE_native_pose.sdf`](../../docking/validation/1XD0_ARE_native_pose.sdf), [atom map](../../docking/validation/1XD0_ARE_atom_map.csv), and [ligand-free receptor coordinate copy](../../structures/proteins/prepared/1XD0_working_no_native_ligand.pdb) retain the experimental ARE pose separately and remove only its 55 atoms from the working receptor. ARE's arithmetic heavy-atom centroid is **(10.441, 15.880, 40.721) Å**. The primary [1XD0 study](https://doi.org/10.1021/bi048334e) treats ARE as an acarbose rearrangement product; a later redocking result would validate only this product's pose under the tested preparation.

All 496 mature-protein residues are modeled in author chain A. The peptide-linked N-terminal pyroglutamate **PCA A1**, NAG A499, **Ca A498**, **Cl A500**, and **142 waters** remain in the working copy. Calcium-coordinating HOH A536 and A546 must be assessed with the calcium coordination shell. Four waters lie within 4 Å of ARE. No alternate locations were detected. The catalytic region includes Asp197, Glu233, and Asp300 ([structure study](https://doi.org/10.1021/bi048334e)). ARE atoms O3A/C3A approach a crystallographic copy of Asp433/Trp434 within 4 Å; the crystal mate is not automatically part of a single-chain GOLD receptor, but this contact limits interpretation of distal-pose RMSD.

Before GOLD use, inspect validation/electron-density information; verify local residue and side-chain completeness; resolve 2QMJ alternates; assign protein protonation and histidine/acidic side-chain states at a declared pH; inspect ligand protonation and generated H positions; decide site-water, NAG, GOL and ion treatment with reasons; and check local sterics after hydrogen addition. Preserve the original receptor and reference SDF untouched, and write any further variants as separate files with checksums. In 1XD0, retain Ca and Cl and their coordination environment unless a documented structural assessment supports a change. For 2QMJ, do not delete nearby NAG or waters by residue class alone. A preliminary PDB receptor and SDF ligand are format candidates for GOLD, but **neither receptor is chemically prepared or validated**. GOLD import, atom typing, site coverage and redocking must still be checked ([CCDC format guidance](https://support.ccdc.cam.ac.uk/support/solutions/articles/103000306413-which-file-formats-does-gold-support-), [CCDC protein preparation guidance](https://support.ccdc.cam.ac.uk/support/solutions/articles/103000306440/)).
