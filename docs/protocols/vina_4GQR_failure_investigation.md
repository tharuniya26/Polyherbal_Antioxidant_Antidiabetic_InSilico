# 4GQR/myricetin failed redocking: receptor chemistry and reference audit

**Step 54 assessment, 13 October 2026.** The 4GQR dry, no-NAG, Ca/Cl/PCA-retaining human pancreatic α-amylase model **failed** the prespecified rank-1, crystal-frame, symmetry-aware MYC heavy-atom RMSD screen (≤2.0 Å): **0/5** independent runs at exhaustiveness 32 (E32) and **0/5** at exhaustiveness 64 (E64). This document investigates that result; it reports no new docking run and does not revise either experiment. The underlying [E32 report](vina_4GQR_redocking_report.md), [E64 assessment](vina_4GQR_convergence_assessment.md), and [validation strategy](vina_validation_strategy.md) remain authoritative records of their settings and outcomes.

## Evidence and audit method

The deposited [4GQR structure](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/4GQR), [MYC ligand-validation page](https://www.rcsb.org/ligand-validation/4gqr/MYC), [full wwPDB validation report](https://files.rcsb.org/validation/view/4gqr_full_validation.pdf), and [primary structural study](https://doi.org/10.1021/jm301273u) were checked alongside the unchanged local PDB/mmCIF, deposited-pose SDF, PDBQT, atom maps, receptor variants, and E32/E64 result files. Local observations below are calculated from deposited or previously prepared coordinates; they are not experimental hydrogen positions. The original 4GQR PDB SHA256 is `e3d36d41f8d59498cb24073997d5ddacf407269b00eef9b6b50c6530ca36b584`; the docked dry receptor PDBQT SHA256 is `71bee218c2cfede049ad8254bc39b33cb7c916055c0355e17236a2227a3e52b4`.

## A. Crystallographic MYC reference

The deposited MYC A504 model has **23/23 heavy atoms**, a single modeled position for every atom (blank alternate-location identifiers), average occupancy **0.40**, and atom B factors approximately **49.51–54.06 Å²**. The [official validation record](https://www.rcsb.org/ligand-validation/4gqr/MYC) reports **RSCC 0.662**, **RSR 0.56**, four bond-length and six bond-angle outliers, zero reported atomic clashes or stereochemical errors, and 100% model completeness. The [full report](https://files.rcsb.org/validation/view/4gqr_full_validation.pdf) specifies, for example, C14–C11 at 1.40 versus expected 1.47 Å (Z −4.82) and O12–C11–C14 at 117.92 versus expected 110.87° (Z +5.03). The deposited pose is therefore usable as a *qualified* reference, but its local geometry and density support are weaker than the nominal 1.20 Å global resolution alone suggests. No alternate MYC conformation was modeled; this does **not** prove that no alternate solution or crystal-bound conformation exists. The density-fit statistics and occupancy do not justify shifting or idealizing the deposited pose to reduce RMSD, and their numerical effect on a 2 Å benchmark cannot be quantified here without a separate crystallographic re-refinement.

The local [PDB-to-SDF atom map](../../docking/validation/vina/4GQR_MYC_atom_map.csv) is bijective for all 23 deposited atom names and elements. The [SDF-to-PDBQT map](../../docking/validation/vina/4GQR_MYC_sdf_to_pdbqt_atom_map.csv) also covers all 23. Reference heavy coordinates agree with the original PDB within 0.001 Å, the SDF and reference PDBQT coincide in the crystal frame, and the RMSD procedure considers the **two chemically allowed graph automorphisms** without fitting each docked ligand independently. There is no observed mapping or coordinate-frame error that explains the roughly 2.5 Å rank-1 displacement. MYC has no assigned stereocentre. This verifies the map implementation, not the precision of the crystallographic ligand pose.

The earlier wet PDBQT placed a MYC hydrogen and HOH A1204 hydrogen only **0.907 Å** apart; its water O–H distances are **1.176–1.190 Å**. That is a clash in a *computed hydrogen orientation*, not evidence that the deposited water oxygen is spurious. Rotating HOH hydrogens could remove this particular H–H overlap, but no hydrogens are experimentally resolved and no jointly optimized, nonclashing water network with a validated Vina treatment has yet been established. Ordinary rigid-receptor Vina fixes each water position; the [Vina hydrated-docking documentation](https://autodock-vina.readthedocs.io/en/stable/docking_hydrated.html) describes a different, specialized method. The E32/E64 receptor omitted these eight waters as an explicitly dry sensitivity model. Restoring the previous wet PDBQT unchanged would import the hydrogen clash and is **not** a defensible next test.

### Catalytic microstates at pH 7.5

Crystal-growth pH **7.5** and the previously prepared OpenMM 8.6.1 receptor gave Asp197⁻, Glu233⁻, Asp300⁻, and neutral His201-HID. Heavy-atom contact checks in the original structure give the nearest MYC–Asp197 OD2 distance **2.403 Å**, MYC–Glu233 OE1 **3.679 Å**, MYC–Asp300 OD2 **5.430 Å**, and MYC–His201 NE2 **5.493 Å**. These distances cannot identify proton donors or acceptors without hydrogen positions and do not prove any catalytic microstate. The pancreatic α-amylase catalytic role of Asp197/Glu233/Asp300 is described in the [structural literature](https://doi.org/10.1021/jm301273u); it does not itself assign their bound-state protonation in 4GQR.

A **PROPKA 3.5.1** assessment was run at pH 7.5 on temporary, unchanged copies of the deposited 4GQR PDB (containing MYC) and the existing local no-NAG prepared PDB (without MYC). The temporary prediction files were not retained, and no source structure was edited. Predicted apparent pKa values, in original/local order, were: **Asp197 2.00/3.90**, **Glu233 8.73/9.29**, **Asp300 5.48/5.74**, and **His201 2.08/−0.08**. The unusually low His201 value and shifts between inputs underscore model sensitivity; these values are computational estimates, not titration measurements. Both inputs predict that **Glu233 may be protonated at pH 7.5**, contrary to the current deprotonated OpenMM assignment. Asp197/Asp300 deprotonation and a neutral His201 remain plausible. PROPKA does not establish the His201 **HID versus HIE** tautomer or an experimentally observed Glu233 hydrogen position. This isolates Glu233 as the clearest one-variable microstate hypothesis; it does not demonstrate that Glu233 protonation caused failed redocking. The installed Meeko 0.8.0 residue template library contains a `GLH` protonated-glutamate template, but a coordinate-preserving, clash-free receptor variant has **not** yet been prepared or accepted by Vina.

The deposited [MYC component](https://www.rcsb.org/ligand/MYC) is neutral myricetin, formula **C15H10O8**; the docked neutral phenolic pattern is therefore a documented reference *model*, not an X-ray protonation measurement. A [primary aqueous preformulation study](https://doi.org/10.1691/ph.2014.3076) reports an apparent myricetin pKa of **6.63 ± 0.09**, suggesting appreciable deprotonation at pH 7.5 in its solution conditions. [Flavonol acidity work](https://doi.org/10.1021/jp4049617) also documents complex aqueous deprotonation and stability behavior. These solution findings do **not** determine which phenol, if any, is deprotonated when MYC binds 4GQR. No charged-MYC docking variant is validated or proposed as part of the single next test.

### Ions, glycan and modified terminus

The deposited Ca A502 is coordinated approximately by Asn100 OD1 (**2.347 Å**), Arg158 backbone O (**2.356 Å**), His201 backbone O (**2.396 Å**), and Asp167 OD1/OD2 (**2.502/2.503 Å**); no original deposited water is within 4 Å of Ca. Cl A503 contacts Arg337, Arg195 and Asn298 atoms at about **3.06–3.33 Å**, plus HOH A1083 O at **3.314 Å**. MYC's nearest distances to Ca and Cl are about **10.14** and **8.49 Å**. The dry PDBQT retains Ca (`+2.000` field), Cl (`−1.000` field), and peptide-linked terminal PCA A1. However, [standard Vina scoring ignores supplied partial charges](https://autodock-vina.readthedocs.io/en/stable/faq.html); PDBQT parsing therefore does not establish correct ionic electrostatics, Ca coordination, or chloride energetics. Keep the ions and PCA in structural models, but do not interpret raw Vina scores as physical affinities. The linked NAG A501/Asn461 was omitted only from the local sensitivity model; its nearest deposited atom is approximately **50.93 Å** from MYC. A direct pocket contact is unlikely on these coordinates, but the full glycosylated receptor has not been evaluated as a Vina model.

## C. Decision and next experiment

**Decision A — one additional controlled diagnostic experiment is scientifically justified, contingent on preparation quality control.** The specifically testable issue is **Glu233 protonation**, supported by two PROPKA input states and the acid/base role of Glu233. The test would compare the current dry, no-NAG, Ca/Cl/PCA-retaining Glu233⁻ receptor with a **new, otherwise identical Glu233H receptor**. Use a validated Meeko `GLH` template; retain all heavy-atom coordinates, other side-chain microstates, ions, PCA, water omission, neutral MYC graph, reference pose, box, Vina scoring, exhaustiveness **64**, 20 requested modes, energy range **5 kcal/mol**, and prespecified seeds **1101, 2202, 3303, 4404, 5505**. Place the Glu233 carboxyl hydrogen only after inspecting local donors/acceptors and rejecting steric clashes; verify atom types, net/formal charge bookkeeping, heavy-atom displacement, PDBQT readback, and Vina input parsing. If a chemically sound variant cannot pass those gates, **do not dock it** and suspend 4GQR at the present failed result. Use the unchanged deposited MYC atom map and two allowed automorphisms for crystal-frame heavy-atom RMSD. Report every run, rank-1 and best-of-all-mode RMSD, score ordering, and failures. A favorable result would establish only **microstate-sensitive diagnostic pose recovery**, not ion-aware scoring, a precise crystal pose, binding affinity, or production readiness. This is a proposed future experiment; **none was performed in Step 54**. No E128 search is justified by the present E64 result.

For a future alternative α-amylase benchmark, [4GQQ](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/4GQQ) is an experimentally determined human pancreatic α-amylase–**ethyl (E)-caffeate (0XR)** complex at 1.35 Å, but the [primary paper](https://doi.org/10.1021/jm301273u) places ethyl caffeate away from the catalytic site and reports active-site disorder. It cannot be substituted as a like-for-like catalytic-site validation reference. [1XH0](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/1XH0) contains an **N298S human pancreatic α-amylase variant** with an acarbose-derived hexasaccharide, not a simpler wild-type small-molecule control. Neither is recommended as immediately ready. If the Glu233H test fails or its preparation gate fails, retain the **negative 4GQR validation conclusion** and search for another experimentally characterized active-site complex or an independently validated ion-aware method before using α-amylase for phytochemical pose analysis.

## Interpretation limits

The strongest direct observations are the E32/E64 failure, official MYC occupancy/density/geometry flags, complete atom mapping, deposited heavy-atom contacts, and ion/water coordinates. The Glu233 and MYC protonation hypotheses, water-hydrogen directions, and physical importance of omitted glycans are **model-dependent**. The study's aqueous plant extracts did not experimentally identify these docked compounds or measure binding to 4GQR. No docking score here is a measured inhibition constant or evidence of antidiabetic efficacy.
