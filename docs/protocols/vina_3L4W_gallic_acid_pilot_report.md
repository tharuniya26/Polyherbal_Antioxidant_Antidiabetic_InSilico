# Step 63 — 3L4W gallic-acid exploratory pose-only pilot

**Decision (15 October 2026): all four combinations are INCONCLUSIVE under the prespecified rank-1 geometric screen.** Twenty of twenty AutoDock Vina 1.2.7 searches finished with confirmed zero exits; none failed or has an unconfirmed exit. All **382** returned modes passed Meeko/RDKit molecular-graph, 12-heavy-atom mapping, formal/partial-charge, coordinate and bond-geometry checks. However, **0/5 rank-1 poses** in every gallic-acid state/receptor pair met the frozen crystallographic miglitol (MIG) pocket rule. All 20 rank-1 poses instead approached the lower **Z− box face** within 0.008–0.067 Å. This is an observed pose-placement and score-ordering limitation of the tested setup, not evidence that gallic acid cannot bind or inhibit the enzyme.

The [20-row results CSV](../../data/metadata/vina_3L4W_gallic_acid_pilot_results.csv) records every seed, command, score, rank-1 geometry, status, hash and error field. The [raw output directory](../../docking/pilot/vina/3L4W_gallic_acid_pose_only/) contains randomized starts, individual multi-mode PDBQT outputs and JSON process logs, the [382-row per-pose table](../../docking/pilot/vina/3L4W_gallic_acid_pose_only/all_pose_metrics.csv), the [four-pair Vina parse preflight](../../docking/pilot/vina/3L4W_gallic_acid_pose_only/vina_pair_parse_preflight.json), the [technical-interruption record](../../docking/pilot/vina/3L4W_gallic_acid_pose_only/interrupted_attempts.json), and the [reproducibility manifest](../../docking/pilot/vina/3L4W_gallic_acid_pose_only/experiment_manifest.json). ## Frozen scientific scope and controls

The [Step 62 input assessment](vina_3L4W_gallic_acid_input_assessment.md) qualified PubChem CID 370 neutral gallic acid (formal charge 0) and one separately prepared carboxylate hypothesis (formal charge −1). The neutral state is a labelled minority-solution sensitivity reference at preparation pH 6.5; the carboxylate is more plausible in bulk water at that pH, while the enzyme-bound microstate is unmeasured. Both were tested against the same two previously assessed **dry/no-NAG** 3L4W receptor hypotheses: Asp443−/Asp542−/His600-HID and Asp443−/Asp542H(OD2)/His600-HID. Neither ligand state was chosen or changed in response to Vina scores.

The Step 62 validator passed all four input records and verified the SHA256 of each SDF, PDBQT, receptor source PDB, Vina executable and native-MIG reference. The [enlarged-box neutral-MIG control](vina_3L4W_large_box_mig_validation.md) retained **10/10** confirmed rank-1 RMSDs ≤2.0 Å across these two receptors; its 10 result/output/log hashes were rechecked, without redocking. Every gallic-acid receptor–ligand pair also passed a separate Vina `--score_only` **input/grid parse** using a seeded start inside the frozen box. Those parse-check scores were not interpreted. The preflight is a technical check, not validation of bound chemistry.

The registered search used the same MIG centroid **(45.38664286, 92.35157143, 34.82200000) Å** and **30 × 24 × 24 Å** box as Step 60. Vina 1.2.7 settings were `vina` scoring, exhaustiveness **64**, `num_modes 20`, `energy_range 5.0`, `min_rmsd 1.0`, one CPU and seeds **1101, 2202, 3303, 4404, 5505** for each of four pairs. The [runner](../../scripts/run_vina_3L4W_gallic_acid_pilot.py) used NumPy PCG64 `SeedSequence(seed, PubChem CID 370, charge code)` to generate an independent random quaternion and translation within ±3 Å, requiring a translation norm ≥2 Å and ≥0.5 Å initial heavy-atom clearance from the box. The **same byte-identical seed-specific ligand start** was used for both receptor states of each ligand microstate. No receptor, ligand, box, scoring or seed parameter was changed after viewing outcomes. One Vina process per worker, at most two concurrent one-CPU searches, avoided the earlier project's four-process memory pressure. The earlier interactive-session interruption left **16 complete zero-exit logs** and **two in-flight attempts without captured exits, stdout/stderr or usable output** (neutral/Asp542H seed 4404 and carboxylate/Asp542H seed 5505). The resumed runner reused those 16 completed runs and executed the four missing seeds, including those two, under the same frozen settings. Their first-attempt exits remain **unconfirmed**, with no recoverable Vina log; the separate technical-interruption record states this evidence gap. **20/20 scheduled scientific runs** have confirmed zero-exit *final* attempts; there were **two additional interrupted technical attempts** with unconfirmed exit and no pose result.

## Pose analysis and predefined decision rule

Every returned PDBQT mode was reconstructed through Meeko/RDKit and compared with its frozen neutral or carboxylate graph. The analysis required the exact 12-heavy-atom map, atom names/types, matching PDBQT partial charges, correct formal-charge sum, finite coordinates, heavy-bond geometry and no internal clash. Gallic acid has no stereocentres. For geometric comparison, the frozen **ten-heavy-atom core** comprises one-based source SDF atoms 1–10: six aromatic carbons, three phenolic oxygens and the carboxyl carbon. The two chemically permitted ring automorphisms were considered without independently superposing docked poses. Carboxyl oxygens were retained in full-atom identity, pocket, face and clash checks but excluded from rigid-core RMSD.

The original [pilot rule](vina_3L4W_phytochemical_pilot_plan.md) was applied unchanged. A rank-1 pose must have all heavy atoms inside the box, core centroid ≤6.0 Å from the deposited MIG heavy centroid, ≥50% of core atoms within 4.0 Å of a deposited MIG heavy atom, no receptor–ligand heavy pair <1.8 Å or van-der-Waals overlap >0.8 Å, and no specified internal nonbonded heavy pair <1.6 Å. It must also be within **2.0 Å unfitted, symmetry-aware core RMSD** of that pair's rank-1 medoid. A pair passes only if **at least 4/5 prespecified rank-1 seeds** pass all these checks. The <1.0 Å box-face flag is reported separately. Catalytic distances to Asp443, Asp542 and His600 are proximity observations, not hydrogen-bond or inhibition assignments.

## Scientific interpretation and next action

The experiment supports the **restricted finding** that standard Vina, with this frozen broad 3L4W search box and two dry receptor microstates, repeatedly **ranks a box-edge, off-MIG-pocket gallic-acid placement first** for both tested charge hypotheses. It also samples lower-ranked MIG-pocket poses. The data do not identify whether broad-box surface competition, the scoring function, receptor water/glycan omissions, protonation, or another modelling factor caused this ordering. The successful native-MIG redocking control shows that the setup can recover *MIG*; it does not guarantee correct placement or ranking for a smaller phenolic acid. Changing the box or selecting lower-ranked modes after seeing scores would be a distinct, preregistered diagnostic, not a reinterpretation of this pilot as a PASS. Neither receptor state passes the present gallic-acid rank-1 screen.

The *Aegle marmelos* gallic-acid occurrence evidence comes from [ethanol-extracted leaves](https://doi.org/10.3390/plants10122573), identified by HPLC-PDA retention-time comparison. **Gallic acid was not analytically established in this project's original aqueous leaf extracts.** A computed placement or Vina score does not demonstrate compound presence, binding, enzyme inhibition, glucose uptake, antioxidant or antidiabetic efficacy, clinical benefit, formulation synergy, or relative potency against mangiferin or quercetin.

**Recommended next executable task:** perform a read-only structural review of the repeated gallic-acid **Z− box-face/off-pocket rank-1 site** against the 3L4W receptor surface and saved rank-2/3 MIG-pocket alternatives, using the present coordinates and unchanged chemistry. Decide *before* any new search whether an independently justified MIG-localized diagnostic box or different validation approach is warranted. For plants not yet represented in the pose pilot, first strengthen the botanical and pH-6.5 chemical evidence for conditional **Boerhavia diffusa** caffeic acid and **Vitex negundo** agnuside; do not launch a broad five-plant docking expansion from the current gallic-acid screen. The receptors still omit linked NAG glycans and active-site waters and retain provisional Asp542/His600 protonation. No ADMET, molecular dynamics or potency ranking was conducted.
