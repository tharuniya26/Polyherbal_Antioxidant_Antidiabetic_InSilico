# Project 4 manuscript figure captions

## Figure 1

**Project 4 analysis workflow and evidential boundaries.** Literature-associated reference structures passed through explicit chemical-input qualification, frozen Vina searches, pose-level geometric auditing and deterministic synthesis. Candidate counts refer to 130 archived searches and 2,559 returned poses. Native controls comprise 40 registered searches plus 10 separate enlarged-box 3L4W MIG searches. PASS required at least four of five qualifying rank-1 poses and is not a statement of binding, inhibition or clinical efficacy.

## Figure 2

**Native-control performance and crystallographic-pose recovery.** (A) Observed rank-1 RMSD ranges and best returned modes for the eight registered control conditions and two separate enlarged-box 3L4W MIG conditions. Horizontal bars span observed rank-1 minima to maxima; white diamonds mark the best returned mode. No midpoint or mean is plotted. The red line marks the prespecified 2.0 Å rank-1 threshold. Every 3L4W condition passed; all three 4GQR conditions failed. (B) Deposited MIG and the rank-1 pose from seed 1101, selected as the median rank-1 RMSD among the five initial 3L4W neutral-MIG runs. The orthographic principal-component projection is a display-only projection of unchanged coordinates; the RMSD calculation used original crystal-frame coordinates without fitting. PDB 3L4W, Asp542− receptor, neutral MIG, seed 1101, rank 1, RMSD 1.8775 Å.

## Figure 3

**Final condition-level geometric classifications.** Every row in the authoritative 26-row synthesis is shown. Bars report the number of five rank-1 poses satisfying all frozen criteria; the dashed line is the required four-of-five threshold. The 10 teal conditions are PASS and the 16 orange conditions are INCONCLUSIVE. Zero-length bars remain labelled so negative results are visible. PASS describes pose geometry only.

## Figure 4

**Representative convergent luteolin pose in the 3L4W MIG pocket.** The displayed pose is the frozen within-condition rank-1 medoid: PDB 3L4W, neutral LIG08, Asp443−/Asp542−/His600-HID receptor, seed 1101, rank 1. Deposited MIG is shown as an unfilled pocket reference. Labels give nearest heavy-atom distances to Asp443, Asp542 and His600; they do not assign hydrogen bonds. Crystallographic waters and N-glycans were omitted from docking. The rank-1 deposited-water-overlap diagnostic was negative. Coordinates were projected without pose fitting.

## Figure 5

**Receptor-, ligand-state- and docking-box sensitivity for mangiferin and quercetin.** All 16 conditions are displayed. The original box was 24 × 18 × 18 Å and the enlarged box was 30 × 24 × 24 Å. The dagger marks conditions containing two complete, chemistry-checked pose outputs whose process exits remain unconfirmed. Classification used geometry, not Vina score.

## Figure 6

**Rutin remains inconclusive across all prespecified seeds.** Rank-1 core-centroid distance to MIG and unfitted core RMSD to the within-state medoid are shown for all five seeds in both receptor states. Squares identify rank-1 poses overlapping a deposited crystallographic-water position within 1.8 Å. The red lines mark the 6.0 Å localization and 2.0 Å convergence thresholds. No favourable subset was selected.
